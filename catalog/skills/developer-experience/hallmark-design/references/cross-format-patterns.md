# Cross-format design patterns

Use this reference when applying hallmark-design to a handbook, presentation, or document export. It owns contextual visual judgment and positive comparisons; the HTML skill owns source coverage, figure fidelity, fit measurements, and playback mechanics. App-specific restrictions do not automatically apply to document formats.

## Choose the composition before the effect

Retain a short visual brief containing audience, reading task, supplied references, actual brand assets, typography, palette, motion purpose, logical slide budget, and two representative compositions. Compare the rendered compositions with the supplied reference before multiplying them across the document. A fresh standalone commission may choose a new visual language; a maintained project handbook retains its established brand and layout unless the user requests redesign.

| Inspected failure | Positive composition to compare | Observable reason it works |
|---|---|---|
| Every slide is a heading above three identical cards | Acquisition: a left-to-right sensor-to-review diagram beside the specific operating caveat | Geometry expresses the real dependency; the caveat stays attached to the relevant step |
| Empty slide with a giant number and no explanation | Comparison: two equally scaled plotted series, a centered external legend, and one sourced interpretation | The visual area carries evidence and the reader can compare equal quantities |
| Dense source table split into extra slides to evade fit | One logical slide with a labeled, keyboard-usable row-group selector and a complete reading table | All rows stay mapped; the selector discloses the within-slide states and preserves the source count |
| Repetitive badges, gradients, separators, and corner decorations | Editorial reading spread with one brand accent at the transition and content-sized section spacing | The accent explains hierarchy rather than announcing every paragraph |
| Correct but visually blank plainness after removing all effects | Branded title with an authentic vector lockup, a purposeful soft-depth figure, and a restrained directional reveal | Identity, hierarchy, and motion serve this source rather than a universal ban |
| Animated line implies observations arrive in an invented order | Comparison marks revealed simultaneously from a shared origin; workflow edges revealed in causal order | Time communicates the meaning of the visual; Replay reproduces it and reduced motion shows the final state |

These are composition examples, not automatic approvals or a fixed template. A particular source may need a different arrangement. Reject filler introduced to hit a numeric fill target. Count covers, dividers and appendices inside the declared source-slide ceiling.

## Brand and transition review

Compare mark contours, wordmark shapes, spacing, aspect ratio, and theme variants with the supplied originals at presentation and small-navigation sizes. A typed wordmark is acceptable only when it is itself the supplied brand source. Preserve the original photograph's subject and resolution; do not synthesize a substitute.

Inspect adjacent reading sections as pairs. Alternate their actual visible order, including banners, independently of saved slide themes. Merge redundant banners while retaining anchors and agenda order. Curves and waves may remain; extend their geometry beyond the viewport and inspect animated extremes so no interior cutoff appears. Headerless presentation layouts still need semantic headings, potentially positioned within the composition rather than a repetitive top bar.

## Verification handoff

Record the reference, what was compared, any rejected candidate with a concrete reason, and the surviving composition. Hand contrast/type/keyboard checks to their owners and report an unavailable delegate as uncovered. Hand every slide and reading section to the HTML skill's rendered fit and fidelity checks. Screenshots, motion states, and final artifact hashes are evidence; a subjective score or an occupied-area percentage alone is not.
