## 5. Plan Discrepancies

- The plan's "credential reuse first" language is implemented as an explicit capability boundary, not an automatic state-database read. This is the approved Phase 4 pre-flight choice and honors the stricter Phase 3 HO-5 contract.
- A default live transport is not shipped because no authorized endpoint/schema probe exists. The provider is fully implemented through injected JSON/HTML transports and fixtures; enabling a concrete transport remains authorization-gated.

---

## 6. Assumptions Made

- **Fixture authority**: Phase 3 fixtures define accepted normalized and semantic HTML behavior; they do not prove live endpoint compatibility.
- **SecretStorage scope**: An explicitly supplied opaque value may be stored, but it remains callback-scoped and never enters settings, Memento, logs, or errors.
- **Cache safety**: Persisted content is untrusted after upgrades or corruption and must pass the normalizer again.
- **CI boundary**: Phase 6's explicit workflow task is the approved point for Node 22 CI and Cursor-profile E2E.
- **UI boundary**: `extension.ts` remains a no-op until Phase 5 owns commands, status bar, panels, warnings, and timers.
