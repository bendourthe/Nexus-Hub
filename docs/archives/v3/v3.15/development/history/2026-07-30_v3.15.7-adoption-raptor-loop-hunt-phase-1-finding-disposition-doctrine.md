## 6. Assumptions Made

- **Confirmed and potential axes may use different CVSS scores**: This follows the plan's requirement to retain a full vector while keeping what is proven separate from what is at stake. The potential score must state its insecure assumption.
- **The higher axis drives prioritization but does not overwrite evidence**: Triage uses the higher rating, while both values remain visible so potential impact is not misrepresented as confirmed fact.
- **No frontmatter sync is required**: Neither skill's YAML frontmatter changed, so hand-editing `data/skills.json` would create unnecessary registry churn.
- **Existing CI is sufficient**: `catalog/skills/**` already triggers the complete non-doc workflow, so another path-filtered job would duplicate coverage and increase runner cost.
- **Unrelated untracked v3.16 files are user-owned**: They were excluded from Phase 1 changes and from only the validator they independently break.
