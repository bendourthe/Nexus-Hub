**Branch**: `feat/adoption-raptor-loop-hunt` | **PR**: N/A | **Merged to**: Not merged

**What happened**: Added `catalog/skills/code-review/security-review/scripts/closure-gate.py` and `references/closure-gate-review-record.md`. The schema inventories components, findings, report claims, and matching facts. The script reports `components_without_review_action_or_caveat`, `findings_without_terminal_or_pending_disposition`, `confirmed_findings_without_supporting_evidence`, `rejected_findings_without_complete_rejection_record`, and `report_claims_without_matching_facts`. ## 6. Assumptions Made

- **Record shape**: A small Nexus-Hub-native JSON document is the correct integration boundary; reproducing another project's directory conventions would add coupling without evidence value.

- **Pending findings**: Only `needs-live-validation` is an explicitly pending disposition, and it must carry the safe-test, vulnerable-response, safe-response, and potential-severity receipt established in Phase 1.
