The un-ticked boxes in six released plans are **exit-checklist items**, not unfinished work: the release and its tag are the completion evidence, and each of those versions passed through `/update release` with its gates.

**Deliberate decision: they were NOT mass-ticked.** An exit checkbox is a verification record. Ticking 144 of them retroactively, without re-running the verification each one asserts, would fabricate a record. That is the same class of dishonesty this release's own known-gaps ledger avoids elsewhere (see QG-5, where a smoke test that was not run is recorded as not run). Logged as a hygiene item instead, resolvable either by re-verifying or by replacing the boxes with a dated "released" note.
