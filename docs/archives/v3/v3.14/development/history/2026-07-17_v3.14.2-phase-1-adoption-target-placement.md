## Context

A real reorganization on 2026-07-16 exposed a convention flaw: comparison reports landed in the wrong version directory relative to the plans they seed. The codex-lb comparison sat under v3.13 while its adoption plan was v3.14; the codesight comparison sat under v3.13 while its plan belonged in v3.15. Both files even documented their own discomfort in prose, the signature of a rule flaw rather than an execution slip. The two files were corrected by hand; this plan fixes the rule. Phase 1 is Fix A (placement by adoption target); Fixes B (from-comparison co-location) and C (drift check + reconcile) follow in Phases 2-3.

## Goal

A comparison declares the release that will adopt it (`Adoption target: vX.Y.Z`) and is versioned and placed by that release, so a comparison authored during one cycle that adopts in a later release lands in the later release's directory rather than the authoring cycle's.
