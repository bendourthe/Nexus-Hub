## Context

Phase 1 (Fix A) made `/compare` record an `Adoption target: vX.Y.Z` field and place a comparison by that target. Phase 2 (Fix B) is the other half of the co-location guarantee: `/plan from-comparison` must READ that field and write the seeded plan into the same version tree, instead of re-resolving a fresh in-flight version. This is defect #2 from the plan overview: the codesight comparison stated target v3.15.0, yet the plan was generated at v3.14.0 because Phase C / Step 4 resolved the version fresh.

## Goal

A from-comparison plan is written into the SAME `version_dir` as its seeding comparison, driven by the comparison's `Adoption target:` field, guaranteeing a comparison and the plan it seeds always live together; a legacy comparison lacking the field degrades gracefully.
