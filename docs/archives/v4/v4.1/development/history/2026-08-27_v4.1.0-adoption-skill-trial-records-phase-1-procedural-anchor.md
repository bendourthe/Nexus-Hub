## 2. Implementation

### 2.1 Catalog authoring rule

`AGENTS.md` now states in three sentences that a SKILL.md body is an operational runbook made of actions, decision rules, artifacts, gates, and observable verification. Supporting knowledge belongs in Tier-3 references. The pointer does not name the source study or copy its percentages.

### 2.2 Owning skill behavior

`skill-create` now requires executable runbook instructions instead of a language or domain textbook, routes supporting pedagogy to `references/`, adds the specified tutorial rationalization, and verifies the runbook shape. `skill-stocktake` keeps directive density advisory and labels mostly expository language- and framework-expert skills as `runbook-backlog` for later remediation without rewriting them or failing validation.

### 2.3 Durable tests and release records

`tests/skills/test_v4_1_skill_mechanics.py` locks the AGENTS.md pointer, the `skill-create` runbook contract, and the advisory stocktake label. `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/todos.md`, `docs/DEVLOG.md`, the plan checklist, the known-gaps ledger, and the docs-cleanup report now reflect Phase 1.
