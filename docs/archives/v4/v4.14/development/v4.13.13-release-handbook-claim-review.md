# Handbook claim review -- v4.13.13 release preparation

**Covers**: the re-attestation of the `distribution` living handbook after the v4.13.13 release preparation changed three of its recorded inputs. **Read by**: the reviewer of the `release/v4.13.13` pull request and `/update release`. **Key topics**: why the evidence went stale, the exact input diff, the claims reviewed, and why the build and render receipts stay valid.

**Candidate**: branch `release/v4.13.13`, created from `origin/develop` at `762c1d757`, with the release preparation commit applied.

## Why the evidence was stale

`check_handbooks.py` reported `distribution: stale evidence -- inputs: configs/platform-defaults.json, scripts/installer.ps1, scripts/installer.sh`. A recomputed snapshot differs from the recorded one in exactly those three digests: `installer.ps1` `11c4f5f8...` to `024251a6...`, `installer.sh` `1b44b824...` to `5c08e680...`, and `platform-defaults.json` `7c5b50ff...` to `3191c740...`. The entry digest and the output digest are unchanged.

## Claims reviewed against the input diff

The whole diff across the three inputs is four lines:

- `scripts/installer.sh`: `NEXUS_HUB_VERSION="4.13.12"` becomes `NEXUS_HUB_VERSION="4.13.13"`.
- `scripts/installer.ps1`: `$script:NexusHubVersion = "4.13.12"` becomes `$script:NexusHubVersion = "4.13.13"`.
- `configs/platform-defaults.json`: the OpenClaw lever's `source_url` moves from `gateway/configuration` to `gateway/configuration/common-tasks`, and its `verified` date moves from `2026-08-30` to `2026-10-15`. The lever keys, the settings and the install target are unchanged.

A search of every distribution source (`source.md` and the five composition fragments) for `version`, `4.13`, `OpenClaw`, `platform-defaults`, `seed`, `source_url` and `lever` returns no match. The only `default` occurrence is the ownership rule "Unowned file: Preserve by default", which concerns owned-file writing.

| Handbook | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| distribution | Installer entry points prepare the bundle and invoke platform integration logic | Holds. Only the version string changed; no delivery call, adapter or copy step moved. |
| distribution | Delivery shapes, name precedence, owned-file writing ("Unowned file: Preserve by default") | Unaffected. No adapter, flattening call, manifest path or ownership rule changed. |
| distribution | Install-time defaults are seeded only where a platform documents a lever | Holds. The OpenClaw change is a citation URL and date; nothing new is seeded. |
| distribution | "Inspect reported actions and actual installed files, then exercise the capability in its host." | Holds. Reporting is untouched. |

No claim is false, and no source edit is needed.
