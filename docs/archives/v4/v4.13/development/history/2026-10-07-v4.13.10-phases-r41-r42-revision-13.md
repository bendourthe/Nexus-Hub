# v4.13.10 Phases R41 and R42 (Revision 13)

Maintainer review: difficulty is better; a player who loses should get at least one retry per level; the passage from level 3 to the Nexus boss should be its own epic sequence, with the background star turning into a supernova that opens a wormhole the ship travels through into the Nexus dimension. Coordinator decision: retries apply to the fixed game only, since the buggy game exists to show that its first hit ends the run.

## Scope

- T123: the first loss on each level of the fixed game (levels 1, 2, and 3, and the boss fight as a level of its own) shows a centred retry card instead of the end card: "Ship Lost", "Retries left: 0", and a "Retry level" button styled like the end card's button.
- T123: a retry restarts the level from its start: a full hull, the chosen ship, the level's starting difficulty, no upgrades, downgrades, or shield, a cleared arena, and the first-shot rules again; on level 3 the boss clock restarts; on the boss level the megaship arrives again with its full entrance and its full 1,625 points.
- T123 score rule (decision): a retry restores the score the level began with, so points from earlier levels are kept and points from the lost attempt are not.
- T123: a second loss on the same level shows the normal end card (its note also reads "Retries left: 0"); each new level grants its own retry; the HUD shows "Retries left: N" small under the score and the map name, and the DOM HUD carries the same count for screen readers.
- T123: the card moves focus to the Retry level button, Enter and Space work on it, and the live region announces the loss, the retry on offer, and the retry itself; the buggy build, and any game with a defect switched on, never offers a retry.
- T124: clearing level 3 plays a 600-tick (10 s) passage instead of the short wormhole: swell (0 to 149: the Ember Belt's star swells and flickers), collapse (150 to 209: it shrinks and turns white-blue), burst (210 to 299: a white flash, an expanding filamented shock ring that vaporises the arena's leftovers, a glowing remnant, and 48 ejecta knots), tear (300 to 359: the shock opens a wormhole whose violet-rimmed mouth grows to fill the view), travel (360 to 519: the ship flies centred and steady through a violet and teal tunnel whose walls stream past), and emerge (520 to 599: the tunnel's exit widens onto the Nexus Storm), then the megaship's entrance.
- T124: the passage ends on the boss's arrival tick, so the boss still arrives 60 s into level 3; level 3 is now 50 s of play and 10 s of passage; the test-only boss jump keeps its short wormhole; the level 1 to 2 and 2 to 3 wormholes are unchanged.
- T124: it shares no element with the level wormhole or the finale: its own palette (`NOVA_LOOK`, used nowhere else in the engine), no swirl disc, arms, streaked stars, dark centre, or spinning ship, and its own frame tags in both renderers; it is skippable like the finale (first to the arrival, then to the entrance), ignores skip input in its first 45 ticks, keeps the ship invulnerable, and shows five still frames under reduced motion.
- New engine hooks: `retry`, `skipNova`, `novaPlan`, and `state()` fields `retries`, `retriesOn`, `retriesLeft`, `retryOffer`, `retryLog`, `levelScore`, `bossLevel`, `nova`, `novaLog`, `transitionInfo.stage`, `hud.retry`, and `hud.nova`, plus the `retry` and `novaStage` events.
- Design: the "Revision 13" section of `docs/releases/v4/v4.13/development/v4.13.10-game-design.md`.

## Troubleshooting

- The first retry probe landed one hit and stopped: the hull's post-hit invulnerability window refused the rest, so the tests step 80 ticks between forced hits.
- The HUD read "Retries left: 1" while the card read "Retries left: 0"; an offered retry now counts as spent, so both read 0.
- The first tunnel captures showed a hard horizontal seam left of the tunnel's centre, where the angle wraps and the wall noise jumped; the noise is now sampled on the circle.
- The first captures also showed the shock ring crossing the arena in under a second, the exit uncovering the whole screen early in the emerge stage, and a faint flash; the ring now spans the burst stage, the exit widens through the whole emerge stage, and the flash is stronger and spreads from the star.
- The bright exit ring washed out the bottom caption; the caption now sits on a dark band.
- The first reduced-motion stills kept the flash in every stage after the burst; it now shows only in the burst still.
- A frozen lancer kept its orange aiming line during the swell, hinting at a shot that cannot come; lancer charges now clear as the passage starts.
- The revision 12 start-screen test failed because the Retry button shared the `.ss-start` class and came first in the actions; it now has its own `.ss-retry` class, with the CSS sharing the button style.
- The new tests first asserted the boss tick after 10-tick steps (3,609, not 3,600) and read the finale before `defeatBoss()` started it; the stage test now records the `bossArrive` event's tick, and the helper reads the state after the defeat.
- No existing test needed a change: the boss still spawns on the same tick and the test-only jump keeps its wormhole.
