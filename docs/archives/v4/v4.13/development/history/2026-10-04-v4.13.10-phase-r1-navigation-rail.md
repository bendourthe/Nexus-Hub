## What this phase was for

The maintainer found the "On this page" chip bar generic and unhelpful because it only appeared at the top. It must follow the reader, and on laptops and monitors it must not take width from the centred content.

## Steps taken

1. **Measured the margin.** The content column is at most 1120 px wide with 24 px of padding. The free space left of the text is 104 px at 1280, 147 at 1366, 184 at 1440, 232 at 1536, and 424 at 1920.
2. **Rebuilt `shared/outline.js` and `shared/outline.css`.** The navigation measures that margin and picks one of three forms, re-measuring on resize and when a hidden page first shows:
    - **Full rail**, for a margin of 184 px or more: a fixed vertical track inside the margin with a reading-progress fill, a node per section, and labels.
    - **Slim rail**, for 56 to 183 px: nodes only, with the current one filled and each label shown on hover or focus.
    - **Compact bar**, below 56 px: sticky under the header, with the current section, a progress line, and a section menu.

    The mount point is `display: contents`, so the bar sticks for the whole column.
3. **Found by testing.** The bar's `display: grid` overrode the menu's `hidden` attribute, so the closed menu stayed open; an explicit rule now hides it.
