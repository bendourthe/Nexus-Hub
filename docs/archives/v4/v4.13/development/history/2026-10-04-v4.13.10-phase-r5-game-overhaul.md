# Session History -- v4.13.10 Phase R5: the game overhaul

**Date**: 2026-10-04
**Plan**: [v4.13.10-training-page-rebuild](../../plans/v4.13.10-training-page-rebuild.md), plan revision after the maintainer review
**Phase**: R5 (T053, T054, T055, T056, T057)

## What this phase was for

The maintainer found the game's graphics disappointing:

- The ships were flat triangles, and every enemy fired the same shot.
- There were too few upgrades.
- The stats sat outside the game.
- Black side bands held stray enemies and rocks.
- Nothing told the player what reaches the next level.

They chose shaded 2D art with a 3D look and raised the Training byte ceiling to 900 KB.

## Steps taken

1. **The world fills the frame.**
    - In landscape the world is 600 units tall and as wide as the arena's shape (960 to 1,500 units), so nothing is drawn in, or wanders into, a band.
    - Phones keep the 720 x 960 portrait world.
2. **Each enemy type has its own weapon.**
    - **Drone**: a fast aimed bolt.
    - **Weaver**: a slow, swaying orb.
    - **Lancer**: charges visibly, then fires a beam down its column.
    - **Bomber** (new on level 3): drops a mine that bursts into six bolts.
3. **Seven upgrades.**
    - **Kept**: shield, twin shot, and extra ship.
    - **New**: spread shot, rapid fire, homing missiles, and a wingman, each timed.
4. **Shaded art.** Hulls with gradient light, canopies, engine glow, and panel lines. The player has three forms, and there are four enemy designs, seven power-up icons, and distinct shot sprites.
5. **In-canvas HUD.**
    - Lives as ship icons, the score, and the level title.
    - A goal line with a progress bar, for example "Survive 1:30 to reach level 2" or "Something big arrives in 0:35", then the boss instructions.
    - Active upgrades with their remaining time, and a banner on level-up.
    - The old HTML stats strip is kept for screen readers only.
6. **Difficulty.**
    - Level 3 was eased after the dodging test bot kept dying: mines burst at a lower speed, and lancers and bombers fire less often.
    - The bot now sees a beam, and a charging lancer, as a threatened column, the way a player reads the glow.
7. **Test hooks.**
    - `spawn(type, x, y)` places one enemy.
    - `dropPowerUp` accepts all seven kinds.
    - `state()` reports upgrade timers, enemy shot kinds, and the HUD goal.
8. **Tests.**
    - Each enemy type fires its own weapon.
    - A lancer's charge is a threat before its beam fires.
    - A mine bursts into six bolts.
    - The four timed upgrades are collected and expire.
    - Spread fires three shots, and rapid fire puts more shots in the air.
    - Unknown upgrades are refused.
    - The HUD goal names the next level and tracks progress.
    - The world matches the canvas shape at four viewports.
9. **Fixes found by the full guide suite.**
    - Two tests still read the pre-R4 story and page (`command` and `.tr-cmd`); they now read `prompt.command` and `.ide-cmd`.
    - The R2 Cheatsheets figure addressed the reader ("you need", "your assistant"), which the guide's voice rule forbids; it now reads "fits the task" and "the assistant".
10. **Byte ceiling.** `TRAINING_CEILING_BYTES` was raised from 400,000 to 900,000, with the maintainer's decision recorded beside it.
