## What this phase was for

Bind every displayed model to the bundled map, and turn the density and scroll-bar requirements into checks against fixed thresholds.

## Phase entry

## Steps taken

1. **T019.** `scripts/stamp_training_models.py` was written in Phase 4, because the badges needed it. This phase adds `tests/guides/test_stamp_training_models.py`. It checks that stamping copies the map and `--check` then passes, that `--check` reports a changed map cell, and that a missing map is refused with nothing written. It also checks refusals for a badge tier the map lacks, for a provider that is not an exact, case-sensitive map column (`openai`, `Mistral`), and for a page without exactly one marker pair. A final check asserts the real page matches the bundled map.
2. **T020.** The Phase 1 empty-rectangle measure became a shared helper, `tests/guides/tools/empty_space.py`, holding the DoD 10 thresholds from the inventory. `tests/guides/test_training_layout.py` measures every Training stage at 1280 and 390 px in both themes, and self-tests the measure against a fixture laid out like the v4.13.4 Training (a card at two-thirds width with nothing beside it), which it must flag. It also checks that the play arena starts within the first 60 percent of a 900 px screen. The scroll-bar rule (no panel scrolls at 1280 px except a diff over 40 lines) was already covered in Phase 4's `test_training_story.py`.
3. **T021.** Both checks passed on the first run, because Phase 4 had already fixed the layout problems the captures exposed (column balancing, the callout beside the banner, the arena height cap). Nothing needed fixing here.
