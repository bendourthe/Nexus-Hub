## Steps taken

1. **Roster.** The enumerator returns the picker sentinel for Claude Code unless `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set, and it was not. With the maintainer's explicit approval, one read-only call to the Anthropic Models API (`GET /v1/models?limit=1000`) used the pilot key mapped into that single command. It returned HTTP 200 with 13 models and `has_more: false`, a complete live roster with provenance `api`. The key was not printed, logged, or written anywhere.
2. **Work-list.** `write_model_prompting_profile.py plan --roster <13 ids>` listed 10 unprofiled models; `--only claude-sonnet-5-5` calibrated on the one this plan covers.
3. **Sources.** Fetched as raw Markdown, HTTPS only, with every final URL re-checked on `platform.claude.com`: the Sonnet 5.5 prompting guide, "What's new in Claude Sonnet 5.5", and the migration guide (27 KB, 22 KB, and 55 KB). Their content was treated as data. They carried no instruction aimed at an agent.
4. **Claims.** Drafted 32 candidate claims, each a paraphrase with a verbatim quote of 300 characters or fewer and the URL. A script confirmed every quote appears verbatim in the fetched page before review.
5. **Adversarial verification** (degradation rung: isolated subagents, three skeptics per round, each refuting from a different angle: source support, currency and conditions, actionability).
    - Round 1 (32 claims): 23 survived unanimously. C14, C23, and C25 failed by majority. C13, C15, C17, C21, C24, and C26 survived 2 of 3 with a valid objection, so they were rewritten rather than recorded weakened.
    - Round 2 (12 rewritten or split claims): 8 unanimous, 2 at 2 of 3 (C15, C34), and 2 failed again (C25, C35) for dropped conditions.
    - Round 3 (3 claims: C25 narrowed, C36 split out for the `strict` limits and Bedrock, C35 rewritten): C25 unanimous; C35 and C36 at 2 of 3.
    - The final payload has 36 claims: 32 `high` and 4 `medium`. Confidence follows the margin. A caveat raised by the lone dissenting skeptic on each `medium` claim is carried in its `note`. Every claim is `model-specific`.
6. **Write.** `write --input verified-payload.json --dry-run`, then the real write. It produced the index entry, `references/models/claude-sonnet-5-5.md`, and the regenerated `references/model-profiles.md`. The primary roster went from 16 to 26 ids (`config` to `api`, last verified 2026-09-30) with none removed; the `codex` platform entry and every other model are unchanged.
7. **Test (T307).** Added `tests/skills/test_prompting_profile_claude_sonnet_5_5.py`. It checks vendor sourcing, scope, and confidence; coverage of the 15 required areas and of each of the five breaking API changes; that the mirror matches the index claim for claim; roster provenance and hash; the planner; the structural gate; and that no shared body names the model.
8. **Sweep (T308).** Report-only: 13 matching lines, two real findings (SW-1, SW-2) in `prompt-engineering/references/step-2-apply-reasoning-techniques.md`, both routed `profile-only` by `apply_prompting_edits.py classify`. Recorded in `development/v4.13.8-stale-example-sweep.md`. No shared body was edited.
