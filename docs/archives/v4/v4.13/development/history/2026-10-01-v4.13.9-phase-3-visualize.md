6. **T415, the tests.** `tests/skills/test_inline_visualization.py` has 21 tests: the skill contract, the command, the template, the produced outputs, and a durable policy check that the skill adds no MCP server, generator script, or installer step. It includes a self-test of the outbound check.

## Deviations

- **Exercise scripts kept as evidence.** The exercise used two evidence-only scripts (`v4.13.9-visualize-exercise/`) rather than an agent working by hand. The skill still ships no generator script, as the plan requires.
- **Junction instead of symlink.** A directory junction stood in for a symlink, because Windows refused symlink creation without developer mode.
- **Durable policy test.** The policy test asserts the durable property (no MCP entry, installer step, or generator script) rather than a diff against this plan's baseline, which would become meaningless after merge.
