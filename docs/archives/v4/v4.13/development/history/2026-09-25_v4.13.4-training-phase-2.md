## 2. Chronological Steps

### 2.1 Enemy archetypes

Tests first failed with `KeyError: 'archetype'`. Four weighted archetypes now supply distinct radii, speed ranges, patterns, projectile specifications, and per-entity look seeds. Their draws use `rngSpawn`; the legacy enemy `band`, spawn `x`, and `vy` projections remain for existing tests. The new archetype tests and arcade baseline passed 37 tests before further Phase 2 edits. Task 2.1 remained uncommitted for the phase gate.

### 2.2 Movement, shots, and beam interval

Tests first failed with `KeyError: 'tracks'` and `KeyError: 'shots'`. New enemies dart, strafe, hold, or weave; Interceptors fire bolts, Gunships fire spreads, Lancers fire a beam with a nonlethal tell and finite active window, and Drones do not fire. The pre-placed teaching enemy keeps its original `rng`-driven cadence. `spawnSample` now advances a scratch state and reports birth-time projections, per-enemy absolute drift, and fired styles without requiring the player to survive a long run. A direct model probe confirms tell safety, active collision, and expiry removal. The five then-current variety tests and arcade baseline passed 39 tests. Task 2.2 remained uncommitted for the phase gate.

### 2.3 Asteroid appearance and fragmentation

Tests first failed with `KeyError: 'vx'` and `KeyError: 'shape'`. Spawned asteroids now have lateral drift, spin, a `look` seed, and a silhouette derived from that seed; the existing asteroid-hit fixture has the same fields. A player shot splits either larger tier into two smaller rocks with distinct fresh look seeds drawn from `rngSpawn`. A model probe verifies two tier-1 children from one tier-2 parent. Ten Phase 2 tests passed, including a deterministic early-versus-late Lancer share check. Task 2.3 remained uncommitted until the full phase gate finished.

- The sample model needed to advance movement and cleanup on a scratch state to truthfully report trajectories and fired shots; birth-time projections were retained separately for baseline consumers.

- Phase 3 currently names enemy, asteroid, and player painters but no projectile painter, even though Phase 2 introduces a visible beam and spread. This omission must be resolved within the approved visual-rebuild scope.
- The plan's illustrative `git add` names the guide and test module only. The one phase commit also carries this release-scoped history, plan progress boxes, and `docs/todos.md`, as required by the repository's progress and evidence conventions.
