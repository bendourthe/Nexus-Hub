## What this round was for

The maintainer found three game problems:

- The fixed game's start screen overflowed its arena with a scroll bar, and it differed from the buggy game's.
- In the buggy game, a hit did not destroy the ship.
- The four ships should differ in style, colour, and attack.

During the round the maintainer also asked for two page changes:

- Big red and green verdict marks in the Training opening.
- A Foundations opening rebuilt as one chat composer sending to a model that visibly works.

## Steps taken

1. **Why a hit did not destroy the buggy ship.** There were two causes.
    - **The self-test blast.** The buggy build's second defect cost 40 health without killing the ship, then opened 60 ticks in which real hits were ignored.
    - **The hit area.** The ship's hit circle (18 units) was far smaller than its drawn wingspan, so shots visibly striking a wing passed through.
2. **The fix.**
    - **Buggy build**: every hit ignores the invulnerability window, the self-test blast takes the same instant-death path, and player collisions use an ellipse matching the drawn wings.
    - **Tests**: a parametrized test places each of 13 real damage sources against a parked ship in the buggy build. It also covers death after Play again, after a ship change, and through the real Start button for each ship.
3. **One start screen.**
    - **Both games**: they share it. It cannot scroll, and it sizes to the measured arena.
    - **Narrow arenas**: ships sit in a 2x2 grid, with an "Upgrade key" button for the second panel; a scale-to-fit fallback covers smaller ones.
    - **Tests**: it fits with nothing clipped at 1280x900, 1440x900, 1920x1080, 1366x768, 1280x720, and 390x844.
4. **A weapon per ship.**
    - **Ships**:

        | Ship | Weapon | Colours |
        |---|---|---|
        | Vanguard | twin heavy cannons | dark steel and cyan |
        | Raptor | three-round burst rifle, forward-swept wings | gunmetal and red |
        | Talon | violet lance beam that cuts through | silver, white, and violet |
        | Specter | homing plasma orbs that split | charcoal and crimson |

    - **Upgrades**: every upgrade stacks on every weapon, and each card names its weapon.
5. **Training opening verdicts.** After the buggy ship is destroyed a big red X stamps over the panel; after the fixed ship takes only 6 damage a big green check stamps over it.
6. **Foundations opening.**
    - **The composer**: one chat box replaces the Tokens, Prompt, and Context cards. Files attach as context; the prompt is typed at reading pace in three highlighted parts (request, goal, format); its tokens and the context bar follow.
    - **The model**: Send hands off to the model, which reads, thinks, and streams an answer, on an 18 second loop.
