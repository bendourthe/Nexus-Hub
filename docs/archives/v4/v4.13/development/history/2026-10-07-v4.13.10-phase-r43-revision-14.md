# v4.13.10 Phase R43 (Revision 14)

Maintainer review: "Almost there, but for the boss, the last couple of nodes (top right and left) are almost impossible to hit." The screenshot showed the Nexus boss high in the arena, its two top nodes partly under the boss life bar, and the text "Break the 1 glowing nodes, then the core" with "Shields down: break the 1 nodes". The maintainer also asked for two rare bonuses: an instant revive and a one-time atomic blast. The coordinator's stated defaults were binding: the revive is held until needed, one at a time, used before the retry, fixed game only; the blast fires once on pickup and never kills the Nexus boss outright.

## Scope

- T125: the megaship turns its whole X frame a quarter turn at a time (rest 330 ticks, turn 90 ticks with easing), so every node swings into the lower half where straight fire reaches it; with no live node low it rests only 60 ticks and turns the way that brings more live nodes down; with only the core left it settles on a half turn so the plates guard the sides and the core stays open from below.
- T125: the megaship sits a quarter of the arena down, or lower when its frame would reach into the boss bar's band, and dips while it turns, so no node rim ever enters the band (desktop rest y 197 of 600, was 150; phone about 400 of 960, was 240).
- T125: both renderers turn the hull, nodes, and glows with the frame; the armour tests use the turned frame (`bossLocal`); the lance scan, the harness, and the finale wreck follow the angle.
- T125: singular and plural: "Break the last glowing node, then the core", "Shields down: break the last node", "Inner shield: the last of 3 layers", "1 shield layer left", "One node left: break the last node"; counts above one are unchanged; the engine sweep found no other count string that can read "1 ...s".
- T125: the harness gains `node_reach` (each live node's share of the node stage reachable along a strip at least 12 units wide, floor 35 percent); its hunt pilot goes under the lowest live node and keeps the R40 standoff from the megaship.
- T125 balance (decision): the turning frame cut the node stage from about 22 s to about 10 s, so the core rose from 200 to 440 (total 1,625 to 1,865), which restored the mean hunt to 64.4 s and narrowed the spread.
- T126: the Revive (`#fde047`): held one at a time (a second one repairs 35 instead); on death the ship bursts and is rebuilt on the spot with a full hull and 120 ticks of invulnerability, with a gold ring, a regrowing ship, a shimmer, and the blink; it answers the loss before the level retry; a "Revive HELD" chip leads the HUD chips and the DOM HUD lists it; games with a defect never hold one and their share drops a Repair.
- T126: the Atomic blast (`#ffedd5`): on pickup a white flash and a shockwave grow from the ship at 16 units a tick across the arena, destroying every enemy (agents included), rock, enemy shot, beam, bomb, and mine its front passes, for normal score; against the megaship it breaks the standing shield layer or takes 40 percent of a node's health from every live node, never the core.
- T126 rarity: both take the last 5 percent of the old Repair share (0.15 to 0.10): 2.5 percent of upgrade picks, about 1.9 percent of all enemy and rock drops, about 1.4 percent of agent drops; every other upgrade drops exactly as before.
- T126: both are in the start screen's strip, key, and details panel (the full key now has six columns, so twelve upgrades fill two rows); the key stays identical in both games.
- Reduced motion: the ship is drawn whole through the revive with a still burst and ring, and the blast front jumps in three still steps that match what it clears.
- Design: the "Revision 14" section of `docs/releases/v4/v4.13/development/v4.13.10-game-design.md`.

## Troubleshooting

- The first reach probe counted any column that met a node, so a top node looked reachable through a 2-unit sliver; the measure now needs an open strip at least 12 units wide, a third of a node.
- With the megaship lowered, the harness's fixed 55 percent line put every pilot closer to the boss and the Warden's short flak gained most (hunt band -38.5 to +42.6 percent, power index 1.114); the pilot now keeps the R40 standoff from the megaship, and the shield stage measured exactly as in revision 12.
- With the nodes reachable the mean hunt fell to 50 s; raising node health would have widened the spread (the Talon is slowest on nodes), so the core took the added health instead (the Talon is fastest on the core), giving a mean of 64.4 s and a band of -26.1 to +22.1 percent.
- A revive granted 120 ticks of invulnerability and the hit that caused it then reset it to 60; a hit now keeps the longer window (`Math.max`).
- The first blast captures showed a frozen frame: the scene had killed the ship before the pickup; the capture scene is now immune.
- The boss bar's last-layer label read "Inner shield: 1 of 3 layers left"; it now reads "Inner shield: the last of 3 layers".
- The new tests first expected a running state, but the tests hold the game paused, and a pinned gunship near the revived ship could hit it as the shimmer ended; the checks now read the paused state with no end reason and use a still asteroid as the nearby object.
- The first full run failed three tests: the HUD chip records gained a `label` field on every chip, which broke the freeze test's exact chip match (only the Revive chip carries a label now), and the per-upgrade collection test indexed `player[kind]`, which the two untimed pickups do not have.
- Existing tests changed because the key deliberately grew from ten upgrades to twelve: `UPGRADES` in `tests/guides/test_training_game_engine.py` (its fit check now counts `len(UPGRADES)`, and its per-upgrade collection test checks the held Revive and the fired blast instead of a timer), and `UPGRADES = 12` in `tests/guides/test_training_game_revision12.py`.
