Every guide heading was renamed to a keyword label; the home tagline is now a paragraph, not a heading.

## Troubleshooting

- **Double rule above H2**: `.fx-scene` and `.tr-head` already drew a rule, so H2 borders were removed inside them.
- **Specificity conflicts**: a scene-level H3 colour, the `[data-ty="h3"]` ink colour, and `.tr-notes-title` overrides each beat the shared rule; fixed at the source instead of adding `!important`.
- **21 test failures after R21**: almost all pinned the old headings, eyebrows, or gradient tagline and were updated to assert the new contract. Two were real defects: a markup class (`fxo-out--li`) with no style rule, and second-person text in the model answer ("after you move out", reworded).
- **Edit guard on scratch builders**: blocked edits to an already-written scratch file; the change was applied through a new builder copy (`r21b_fxo.py`) and directly to `opening.css`.
