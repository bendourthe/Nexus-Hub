## What this phase was for

A from-scratch, deterministic game engine that fills the content width, carries the two seeded bugs as independent flags, and exposes clean interfaces to the story and to tests, drawn with simple shapes for now.

## Steps taken

1. **T010.** Recorded the engine design in [`v4.13.10-game-design.md`](../v4.13.10-game-design.md): a fixed 60 Hz simulation separate from rendering, three seeded random streams (spawn, drops, defects), every timing rule in ticks, logical worlds of 960 x 600 (landscape) and 720 x 960 (portrait), the story interface (`configure`, `start`, `pause`, events), the test layer (`manual`, `step`, `input`, `state`, `forceHit`, `threats: false`, the pure `defectSchedule`), and the test rules.
2. **T011.** Wrote the engine as its own source file, `guides/website/src/sky-sentinel.js`, inlined into `training.html` by `stamp_guide_shared.py` as a Training-only entry. Each play stage hosts one game: buggy (both defects), partly fixed (random explosion only), and fixed (neither). The engine handles keyboard, mouse, and touch. It pauses and releases held keys when the arena is under 30 percent visible, the tab hides, the window loses focus, Escape is pressed, or the stage changes. Its HUD is HTML with a polite live region, and a text fallback appears when a canvas cannot be drawn.
3. **T012.** No enemy fires for 180 ticks, and the first shot leaves from at least 300 world units above the ship. `firstHitFatal` ends the run on the first hit with lives left. `randomExplosion` costs a life with no hit, first at tick 1,200 or later, never within 900 ticks of the last one, and never while the ship is exploding or respawning. With neither flag, a hit costs one life and gives 120 ticks of invulnerability.
4. **Fixed by rendering.** A phone capture showed the portrait arena letterboxed into a landscape-height canvas. The play stages are hidden at load, so the engine measured 0 px and assumed landscape, and a later reset never re-fitted the canvas. The engine now falls back to the viewport width, re-fits when the world's shape changes, and re-chooses the shape while a game is still idle. A regression test covers the exact path (load on the intro, then open a play stage on a phone).
5. **T013.** Added `tests/guides/test_training_game_engine.py`: determinism, the grace and first-shot clearance across five seeds, each defect alone and both together, the explosion bounds over 400 seeds through the pure scheduler, the schedule's independence from spawns, a rejected unknown defect, scripted and real-key input, cancellation of left plus right, the hidden-tab and scrolled-away pauses, resize without reset, device-pixel-ratio backing, touch controls with 44 px targets, the portrait phone arena, and the canvas fallback.
