## Commit scope and hostile arguments

`v4.13.9-review-drafts-exercise/commit_scope_check.py` applies the documented procedure:

1. Validate against `^[0-9a-fA-F]{7,40}$`.
2. Resolve with `git rev-parse --verify --end-of-options "<arg>^{commit}"`.
3. Read the parents with `git rev-list --parents -n 1 --end-of-options <sha>`.
4. Pick the diff.
