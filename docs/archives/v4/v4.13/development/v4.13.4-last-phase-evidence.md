## Human/manual testing suggestions

- Play all three games on a touch device and confirm the on-screen buttons move, fire, and pause each game without affecting the others.
- Navigate the seven sections with only a keyboard and a screen reader, and confirm each game's live region announces lives and state changes once, not per frame.
- At native browser zoom of 200% on a 1280-pixel-wide window, check whether the header navigation overflows (this decides WN-1).
- Ask a reader new to Nexus-Hub to follow the sections in order and say where the story from bug to fix to release stops making sense (this feeds the open v4.2 DF-2 workshop).
