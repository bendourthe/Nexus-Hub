## Sub-task 6.1 -- Measurement before CSS

[`presentation-geometry.md`](../guide-production-ready-rebuild/presentation-geometry.md) records the v4.4.1 baseline from the browser, not from a screenshot: the arena was a 213 px column with the stage at 10 to 26 percent of the viewport height, the terminal's tools and artifacts sat below the fold at every one of the four sizes, and a `max-width: 1600px` inherited from the in-page layout left 182 px of dead margin on each side of a fullscreen 1920. Three structural faults, none cosmetic.

The same document states the acceptance numbers. The coverage floor is 0.88. The stage-height floor is 0.45, not the plan's 0.70: with the toolbar, progress strip, head, takeaway, and controls all visible (which the Definition of Done also requires) the arithmetic shows 0.70 is unreachable even at 1080 px, and the deviation is recorded in the contract rather than hidden behind a loosened test.

## Sub-task 6.2 -- The layout

`.nht-grid` becomes `display: contents` in present mode, so its two columns and the sibling explorer are direct items of a three-track slide grid (`minmax(300px, 34fr) 33fr 33fr`, rows `auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto auto`, no `max-width`). Every pane's height is the body row's definite track, which is the `BG-11` lesson applied deliberately: no fill-sized child inside a content-sized parent, so nothing can latch to min-content after a reflow. Inside the arena pane the stage derives its width from the available height through its 3:4 ratio; inside the evidence pane the terminal fills what tools and artifacts leave and its output is the one permitted secondary scroll; the explorer stacks its tree above the file body and the body scrolls. The 58 percent column rule from v4.4.1 is deleted.

Outline is `position: absolute` below the bar in present mode, closing the carried v4.4.1 P3: opening it changes no region rectangle, proven at all four sizes. A `matchMedia` change listener on the layout query closes it and refocuses the trigger if the window crosses a breakpoint while present, so the overlay can never be stranded across the two layouts. The narrow reflow is now keyed on `(max-width: 1023px), (max-height: 639px)`, so a wide but short window reflows into one scroll surface exactly as a narrow one does.

The first build reached 0.42 stage height at 1280x720 against the 0.45 floor. Rather than lower the floor, a `(min-width: 1024px) and (min-height: 640px) and (max-height: 768px)` rule clamps the head, intent, and takeaway to one line and tightens the arena chrome; the stage rose to 0.47.

## Sub-task 6.3 -- Tests

The seven v4.4.1 tests (control placement, label state, nonmodal Outline, Escape precedence, desktop intersection, narrow reflow, route change) pass unchanged, which is the proof that the rebuild kept every earlier contract.
