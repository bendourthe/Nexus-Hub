## What was wrong, and it was mine

v4.4.3 gave Foundations the same pattern as Home: an uppercase label above the heading. On Home that
label is a CATEGORY ("Guardrails & Safety"), so it belongs above. On Foundations the phrase is a
DESCRIPTION of the scene ("The units a model actually reads"), and the scene name is what the reader
is looking for. Putting the description above the name inverted the pair, and the review said so.

The fix is the swap plus a restyle, because a subtitle is not a label: the phrase drops its uppercase
treatment, its letter-spacing, and its accent dash, and renders at 17px in dimmed ink under the
title. It also leaves the no-wrap fit pass, since a sentence should wrap like prose rather than
shrink to hold one line.

## Two contracts retired, both registered

- `test_scene_subtitles_are_eyebrows_above_their_titles` asserted exactly the arrangement the review
  rejected. It is rewritten as `test_scene_titles_come_before_their_subtitles`, asserting the h2
  first in document order AND on screen, a subtitle smaller than its title, and
  `text-transform: none` so it cannot drift back into being a label.
- The heading module measured `.fx-subtitle` for no-wrap and floor compliance. It no longer should,
  and its docstring says why. Titles and Home labels are still measured.

Home is untouched and still reads label-above-title, which is asserted in the Phase 1 and 2 module.
