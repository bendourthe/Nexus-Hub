## Goal

The third review round asks for two different kinds of change, and the plan keeps them apart.

The first is a set of precise corrections to scenes that already work: stop an animation that made a figure hard to read, rename labels, re-lay out two figures, and double the size of the small uppercase tags on Foundations.

The second is larger. The operator supplied THREE external HTML mockups and asked that Models, Agentic Platforms, and Harnesses be rebuilt from them, integrated into this guide's design system, and compacted. Those mockups are standalone multi-section pages built around interactive controls (tabs, selects, run buttons). This guide is one offline file with a byte ceiling, a no-second-person rule, and a reduced-motion contract, and the same review round explicitly asks for FEWER hidden panels, not more. So each mockup is treated as a source of teaching structure and visual ideas, not as markup to paste: its interactive states become always-visible content, its eight or three sections become one compact scene, and its second person is rewritten.

## Non-goals

Training and the arcade remain out of scope until the operator has reviewed Home and Foundations. No engine rewrite. No new external subresource, and no change to the byte ceiling or the offline guarantee. The mockups' navigation bars, hero sections, footers, and skip links are not imported: this guide already has all of those, one level up.

## Risks

**The byte ceiling is the real constraint this time.** Three full scene rebuilds could add 40 KB or more if the mockups' generous spacing and long prose come with them. The mitigation is structural rather than hopeful: Phase 1 hoists four duplicated vendor marks into shared symbols (a net saving), each rebuilt scene replaces markup rather than adding to it, and the ledger is measured at every phase instead of at the end.

**A mockup's interactivity is load-bearing for its teaching.** The permission boundary in the agentic mockup teaches by showing a blocked step. Removing the button must not remove the lesson, so the blocked state becomes a visible outcome rather than a hidden one.

**Doubling every tag can break a narrow layout.** A 24px uppercase tag with letter spacing is wide. Phase 2 sweeps five widths before committing, and the no-wrap engine is measured rather than trusted.
