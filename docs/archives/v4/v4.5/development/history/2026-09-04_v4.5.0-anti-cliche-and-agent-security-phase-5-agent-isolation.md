## Outcome

`agent-execution-isolation` now closes the four boundary gaps the incident evidence exposed. An isolation design built from it would treat an allowlisted internal service as a node with its own reachability rather than a leaf, would refuse to call two sessions isolated while they share a writable service, would harden the host on the assumption the sandbox eventually fails, and would minimize the sandbox's own devices and interfaces rather than only the software inside it. The three-layer model became four, the guardrails-versus-boundaries principle and the agents-do-not-stop framing are named at the top of the Instructions, and egress is enforced at more than one layer.
