This record covers T021-T024 of the v4.10.1 eval-isolation and adaptive-compaction plan. ## Completed work

- Added a five-dimension 100-point rubric: correctness 35, autonomous completion 20, actionability 20, safety 15, and concision 10. Safety is weighted above the source pattern because Nexus-Hub guidance governs tool-using agents; correctness remains the largest dimension.

- Added a fail-closed release-gate conjunction with a numeric 2-point tolerance, a hard blocker veto, missing-score and missing-condition exclusion, a strict weighted improvement requirement, and identical cases/model/trials/rubric provenance for published comparisons.

- Replaced delegated random shuffling with an exact SHA-256 contract over `group_key + NUL + condition_name`, digest ordering, lexical collision tie-breaking, persisted `blind-map.json`, and resume-time map verification.

- Required one comparator call per complete case-and-trial group. An incomplete group is written to stderr, receives an excluded receipt, and is not judged.

- Added explicit judge-region markers around the only rubric text sent to the blind grader. Condition names and release-gate vocabulary remain outside the region.

- Added `references/headroom-estimation.md` with a six-step perfect-information oracle method, the source S15 11.5-point worked result and its fixed-opportunity qualification, and the distinction between ceiling estimation and regression floors.
