1. What does the model do, what does the platform provide, and what does Nexus Hub add?
2. Why might a long conversation leave less room for an answer? Is one word always one token?
3. Improve this request: "Summarize the project".
4. For a weekly status brief, which two documents would help, and which material would you leave out?
5. Give a reason a draft/check/fix loop should stop without claiming success.
6. If two branches extract different information, when may their results be combined? What happens if one fails?

Use a new project example for questions 3-6 to check transfer rather than recall. Record each answer as clear, partly clear, or unclear with the reader's words. Any unclear essential concept needs an owned correction and another affected visual/behavior check before publication.
