## Sub-task 7.2 -- Title scale and README

The Phase 1 token started at 3, the operator's "triple for now". The tuning rule was fixed in the plan: reduce the token (floor 2.4) if any title wraps to three or more lines at 1440 or exceeds 40 percent of the viewport height at 420. Measurement found exactly one violation: `Prompt Engineering` wrapped to three lines because the word `Engineering` at 82 px cannot fit a Foundations copy column. At 2.4 (65.3 px, still 2.4 times v4.4.1) every title on every page is two lines or fewer at 1440 and at most 12 percent of the viewport at 420. The Phase 1 size assertion moved with it, as a recorded register row.
