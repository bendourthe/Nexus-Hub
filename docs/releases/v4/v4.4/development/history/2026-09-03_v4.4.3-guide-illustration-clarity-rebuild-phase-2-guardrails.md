## Why it was messy, precisely

The operator's screenshot showed labels wider than their chips and hook names sitting on top of the
rings. The cause is in the construction, not the coordinates: every label was an SVG `<text>` at a
fixed `10.5px` inside a `0 0 420 260` viewBox that scales with the column. SVG text does not wrap,
does not shrink, and does not know how wide the `<rect>` behind it is, so `commit + key` at 10.5px
mono is simply wider than an 86-unit chip and the two are drawn independently. Nudging coordinates
would have moved the collision, not removed the possibility of one.

There was also a genuine markup defect underneath: the `data-seq="3"` group was never closed, so the
fourth and fifth steps were nested inside the third. That is the "misplaced content" in the review.

## What replaced it

HTML boxes that size to their own text, which is the property SVG text lacks:

- **The rings** are two nested elements. The outer one is the Nexus Hub hook ring (dashed, accent),
  the inner one the platform permission ring, with the model as the innermost box. Nesting is now a
  fact of the DOM rather than four hand-placed rectangles, and the test asserts containment
  geometrically.
- **The four hook names** are chips inside the outer ring, wrapping as a normal list.
- **The evidence** is three lanes, each a four-cell row: the attempted action, what the platform ring
  did, what the Nexus Hub ring did, and the result. Every lane is *allowed at the prompt*, which is
  the entire argument of the section, and each blocked lane names the hook in bold. Below 700px the
  lanes reflow to two columns and each cell prints its own column label.

The section's own claim is unchanged and still verified against the shipped catalog: only hooks that
exist as both `.sh` and `.ps1` and are registered in `settings.json` may be named.

## Two v4.4.1 assertions repointed, both registered

`test_guardrails_section_names_only_shipped_registered_hooks` read `.g-port` and a `blocked by `
prefix; `test_guardrails_choreography_reaches_a_fully_blocked_end_state` pinned `total == 5` and
counted `.g-block` paths. Both classes are gone with the SVG. The rules they enforce are not, so
both were repointed at the new carriers and both rows are in the register. Nothing was loosened: the
first still proves every named hook ships and is registered, the second still proves the choreography
ends with two blocked attempts.
