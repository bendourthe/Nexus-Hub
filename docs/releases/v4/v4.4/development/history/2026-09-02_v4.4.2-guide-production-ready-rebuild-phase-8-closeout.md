## Two lessons this plan added to the ledger

- A media query adds no specificity and a later rule of equal specificity wins on source order. This bit v4.4.1 twice (`BG-4`, `BG-11`) and v4.4.2 twice more (Phase 3's `.fx-stack` and `.fx-subtitle`); the fix each time was a compound selector with a comment saying why. The rule is now in the README.
- Text carriers never lose contrast during a sequence. `BG-13` (dimmed loop pills) and `BG-14` (dimmed ghost chip) were both caught by the contrast sweep, and both were fixed by marking the active or ghost state with a ring, tint, or dashed stroke rather than opacity.
