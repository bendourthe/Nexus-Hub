## Sub-task 1.2 -- NexusSeq

`window.NexusSeq` is one ES5 module (about 3.9 KB) with a declarative contract: a `[data-seq-root]` holds `[data-seq=N]` steps, each gaining `is-on` in order after its `data-seq-dur`; `data-seq-loop` repeats after `data-seq-hold`. It starts at 40 percent visibility, pauses offscreen and on `visibilitychange` hidden, resumes from the SAME step, ignores a second start while running, skips a malformed step with a `console.warn` naming the element and keeps going, starts immediately when `IntersectionObserver` is absent, and under reduced motion `play()` applies the terminal state synchronously and schedules nothing. `play`, `pause`, `reset`, `register`, and `state` are exposed for tests. The demonstration root is the Home workflow loop.

## Sub-task 1.3 -- Section-title scale

One token, `--title-scale: 3`, drives `.section-title`, applied to all 20 section h2s (Home 4, the Foundations bridge 1, Cheatsheets 7, the eight Foundations scene titles). The Training slide title is deliberately excluded: it is a slide title, not a section title. At 1440 px every section title renders at 81.6 px, exactly three times v4.4.1's 27.2 px. One deviation from the plan's literal formula is recorded in the contract: the minimum bound is 0.6x the scaled floor (34.5 px) rather than 57.6 px, because at 57.6 px a single long word overflows a 320 px viewport; the 1440 px anchor the Definition of Done names is unchanged.

## Defect BG-13: the sequencer demo failed WCAG AA

The first demo dimmed inactive loop pills to opacity .45 with `seq-fade`. The v4.4.0 contrast sweep, which samples every visible element at six widths in both themes, caught the pills at 1.9:1 mid-sequence in the light theme and 3.4:1 in dark. The fix is a design rule, not a number: TEXT carriers never change foreground contrast during a sequence. A new `seq-glow` primitive marks the active step with an accent ring and glow; `seq-fade` keeps a .7 floor and a comment reserving it for shapes and connectors. The sweep passes again.
