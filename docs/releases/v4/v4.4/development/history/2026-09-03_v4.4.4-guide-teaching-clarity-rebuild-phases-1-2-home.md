## One rule narrowed rather than raised

Adding the figure pushed the section to 340 words against a 266 ceiling, from the v4.4.1 rule that a
restored Home section may be at most two thirds of its v4.1.2 word count. That rule exists so a
restoration cannot reintroduce v4.1.2's length. This content was never in v4.1.2, so measuring it
against that baseline measures the wrong thing.

The fix is narrow and it is recorded in the phase contract. Blocks marked `data-v444-new` are
excluded from the restored-prose count, and the new block carries its own explicit cap of 20 to 120
words. Restored prose still cannot creep back, and the new figure cannot grow without a number on
it. The restored prose then measured 267 against 266, one word over, so the lead was trimmed to 262
rather than the ceiling being moved again.
