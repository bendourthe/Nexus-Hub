## Sub-task 5.2 -- The engine

**Dual-stream seed.** `rng = mulberry32(seed)` is the BEAT stream and is consumed exactly as v4.4.1 consumed it (starfield, the pre-placed enemy's re-fire cadence, the `play` fixture's timers). `rngSpawn = mulberry32(seed ^ 0x9E3779B9)` drives every new spawn decision. Adding spawns therefore cannot shift a draw the beats depend on, and the two beat tests (`buggy` first hit before tick 120 with lives intact; `fixed` walk `3 -> 2 -> 1 -> 0` with the 90-tick window) passed without a single edit, which is the proof the plan asked for.

**Continuous, varied spawning in every fixture.** Enemies arrive every 90..150 ticks in one of three velocity bands (`40..55`, `60..80`, `90..115` px/s) with an engine glow tinted by band; asteroids every 70..120 ticks in one of three size tiers (`r 8..11`, `12..17`, `18..26`, a large rock gaining a third crater) at one of three speeds (`45`, `75`, `110`). `play` spawns across the full width from tick 0. The teaching fixtures spawn from tick 120, after the buggy hit has landed, and keep the centre band `[135, 225]` clear with `vx = 0`, so a stationary player at `x = 180` receives exactly the seeded beats while a player who moves is fair game.

**Pointer contract.** A primary `pointerdown` inside the stage fires with the same 14-tick cooldown as Space; it starts nothing while `idle` (the start button owns activation) and resumes nothing while `paused` (Resume owns that), so a stray click can never change lifecycle. `pointerleave` pauses with reason `pointer` and releases held keys; `pointerenter` does not resume; the Resume control clears `pointer` and `manual` together. Secondary buttons are ignored and the context menu is suppressed inside the stage.

**Key guide.** Under `(pointer: fine)` a `role="list"` of `kbd` hints replaces the touch buttons (`Left / Right or A / D move`, `Space or click fire`, `Up / Down or W / S move vertically after the feature`, `Esc or leave the arena pauses`); under `(pointer: coarse)` the five labelled touch controls remain. `Pause / Resume`, `Reset demo`, and the reduced-motion `Advance one step` moved beside the HUD on every pointer type. The start hint, the canvas label, and the instructions paragraph were updated to match.

**A pure seam for variety.** `logic.spawnSample(fixture, seed, ticks)` runs `spawnStep` alone on a scratch state and returns the spawn sequence, so tier variety is testable without the player surviving it. This exists because the first live variety test killed its stationary player after three enemies, which is correct behaviour in `play` and not the property under test.
