## Goal

Every illustration on Home and Foundations reads correctly on first look, with no text escaping its container, no decorative shape that represents nothing, and no scene that wastes its width. Section headings follow one stated size rule. The product never addresses the reader in the second person.

The trigger is a direct visual review of the shipped v4.4.2 build across ten screenshots. Each item below traces to one of them.

## Non-goals

Training and the arcade are explicitly out of scope this version; the operator reviews Home and Foundations first. No engine rewrite of `NexusSeq`; no new page; no change to the byte ceiling or the offline guarantee.
