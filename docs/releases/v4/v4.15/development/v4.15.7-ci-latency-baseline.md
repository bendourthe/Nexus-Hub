Result: of 10 distinct plan branches, 3 were red on the first attempt (v4.14.7, v4.14.6, v4.14.4; 30 percent) and 1 was cancelled (v4.14); 6 were green. Across the 14 pull requests, 7 were not green on the first attempt (6 failure, 1 cancelled), because three branches opened two pull requests each. Every red case failed in a job the fast profile does not run (`tests`, `tests-windows`, `tests-windows-installer`, install-smoke) or in `validate`. The fast profile can catch only the `validate` kind of failure, so the pre-push gate addresses a minority of first-attempt reds (1 of 3 red branches named `validate`). That is the evidence-based value of Phase 4's gate: cheap feedback for the policy checks, not a substitute for the test jobs.
