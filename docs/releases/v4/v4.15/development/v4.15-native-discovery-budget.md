## Correction Boundary

The correction is confined to repository source and focused regression coverage. An explicit batch reader may reuse only provenance metadata for its exact context, parent, and enumerated literal paths. It must observe repository identity and tracked membership before and after the batch, read record bytes freshly, and return no discovered authority if either observation fails or changes. A reader may not escape its own active batch. Standalone reads, record parsing, signatures, malformed-record handling, duplicate-authority detection, and fresh lease validation retain their existing behavior.

Installed helpers and real run records are not development targets. Delivery requires green protected integration and exact merged bytes with verified backups. A passing source test alone does not establish the installed handoff or minor completion.
