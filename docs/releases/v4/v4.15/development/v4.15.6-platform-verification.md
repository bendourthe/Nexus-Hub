# v4.15.6 Platform Verification

Summary: release-time record for v4.15.6 that re-stamps the platform read contract from the v4.15.2 verification of 2026-11-16. No new vendor verification was performed for this release.

## Method

The [v4.15.2 platform verification](v4.15.2-platform-verification.md) re-fetched all 65 first-party URLs of the 17 integration groups and found no changed discovery path or format (3 groups unchanged, 13 unchanged with additive observations or wording changes, 1 unverifiable as recorded, none drifted). In the `v4.15.5..develop` range, the Antigravity integration registers the `worktree-policy` hook in its `run_command` group and an `implementation-state` consumer on `Stop`, and the Cursor integration registers an `implementation-state` consumer; the installers copy the central worktree helper and the new hook files. These are hook registrations on paths and events the contract already covers. None adds or moves a platform read path, changes a file format, or touches a lever row.

## Result

`docs/policy/platform-read-contracts.json` is re-stamped with `last_verified` 2026-11-27 and `verified_for_version` 4.15.6. All verdicts, drift and unverified findings from the v4.15.2 record carry forward unchanged, as do its reading limits and the advisory lever review. No vendor application was launched or authenticated, so this record does not show that any platform consumed a Nexus-Hub file, and the Antigravity and Cursor hook registrations named above were not checked against the vendors' current hook documents.
