## Rationale

The completion checker publishes the members of a minor run in version order, so a plan numbered v4.15.12 ships after every other v4.15 plan, however early it is implemented. The maintainer wants the status command and the footer immediately, and the plan touches no file that another queued plan owns apart from one runbook line that v4.15.4 absorbs. Folding it into v4.15.1, the next release, gives the maintainer both without renumbering any plan, and it removes a plan, a worktree, and a pipeline run. The decision is recorded in `docs/decisions/implemented/process/2026-11-13-release-trains-and-standalone-infrastructure-prs.md`.
