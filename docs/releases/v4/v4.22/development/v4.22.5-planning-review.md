## Resolved Design Frontier

### D1 - QA Reach

The preset needs analysis-logic, but existing product-pulse installations may not contain that owner. The user selected "New product-review preset only (Recommended)" on 2026-10-21. The selected scope makes numerical QA mandatory in product-review and available directly through analysis-logic, while retaining standalone pulse behavior. This limits the change to the new workflow and its complete bundle.

The alternative makes QA mandatory for every pulse invocation. That changes an existing workflow and requires broader bundle/module dependency closure, compatibility cases and exact source claims. Doing neither leaves the new workflow without its required QA gate and is not a complete adoption of A1/A2. The question is scope, not a missing fact about the repository.

### D2 - Behavioral-Test Allocation

The user selected "19 synthetic test episodes (Recommended)" on 2026-10-21. The allocation is 19 synthetic test episodes on the existing configured account and one pinned model: one installed QA smoke in Phase 2, eight paired baseline/candidate cases in Phase 3, and two final held-out checks for complete preset discovery/brief and absent-owner behavior. This counts dedicated tests, not all implementation/reviewer reasoning, and promises no dollar ceiling. It introduces no new service or runner and does not authorize enabling pay-per-use.

A smaller smoke-only exercise leaves behavioral acceptance incomplete. If the native host cannot demonstrate complete resource loading, isolated configuration, practical termination and required action evidence, the affected trials do not launch and the acceptance claim stays blocked. A dedicated runner is outside the agreed construction ceiling and requires a different scope decision.
