### Scenario exercise

Exercised through `[[functional-verification]]` by a fresh agent given only `model-routing/SKILL.md`, `effort-modifiers.md`, and the runbook's Phase 6, at tier `strong`, effort `high`:

- **Sanitizer, two new failing inputs after each of two fixes**: classified `IMPL`, edge-case miss, at the end of iteration 2, quoting the inputs fixed and the new ones; offered option D, raise effort one level, confirm first.
- **Migration test, identical assertion after two fixes**: classified `IMPL`, wrong approach, quoting the repeated `expected 120 rows, got 0`; offered option D as a tier upshift to `frontier`, a re-plan, or a question, and explicitly not an effort raise; confirm first.

Both match the Verification Expectation. The agent flagged two points the text left open: whether option D is offered at iteration 2 or with the exhaustion menu, and whether "one level" follows the portable vocabulary or Claude Code's five-value knob. `effort-modifiers.md` now states both: the iteration-2 class guides the iteration-3 fix and D is offered with the exhaustion menu; one level is the next portable value, mapped to the host knob by Step 6.
