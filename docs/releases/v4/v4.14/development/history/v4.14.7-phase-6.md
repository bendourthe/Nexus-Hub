## Plan Delta

**Disposition: Incomplete (non-blocking).**

Evidence: the Phase 6 Goal says legacy trees are "reported by install and `doctor`". Install output is rendered by the two installer scripts, and decision D8 limits installer edits to the forced pass-through and the evidence lines; adding a legacy section there would exceed it. Legacy trees are reported by `doctor` and `prune-undetected`. Separately, the snapshot taken during classification uses `os.lstat`, because on Windows a `scandir` entry reports inode 0 and every pre-unlink re-check would otherwise read as a change.

Consequence for remaining phases: Phase 7's Goal review records the install-output reporting as covered by `doctor` under D8.
