## Implementation

`quality-gate-definitions/SKILL.md` now has five outcomes with the precedence FAIL (blocking) > FAIL (fixable) > UNGATED > PARTIAL > PASS. UNGATED covers a gate with no required criteria, an automatic criterion with no value or a zero denominator, a manual criterion with no approver and date, a `pass^k` criterion with fewer than k trials, and a check that could not run. An unmeasured optional criterion yields PARTIAL. The reason is one of seven fixed categories, never raw error text. A required criterion measured earlier and not now is reported as a regression. The behavior template gained an `On UNGATED` block, and a worked manual-criterion example (`plan-approved`) shows PASS, UNGATED, and FAIL (fixable).

A new "After UNGATED or exhausted FAIL" section holds the explain-retry-or-record flow in one place: a five-line plain-words message, at most two further attempts, the agent records the `QG` gap itself after that, an unattended run records it immediately, and only the user accepts a gap interactively. Missing-evidence reporting is handed to `[[verification-before-completion]]` in one sentence. The runbook's troubleshooting step (Phase 6) hands off to the new section in one sentence and restates none of it.

The body was 464 lines at the baseline and is 497 now, under the 500-line target. To make room, the "What This Skill Does" list and the opening of the Merge-Readiness section were condensed with no link or rule dropped.
