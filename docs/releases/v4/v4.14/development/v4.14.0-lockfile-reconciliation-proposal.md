## Decision

It discards only the verified npm metadata side effects and returns those files to their retained working-tree bytes, including their original CRLF line endings. Keeping the generated metadata would publish an unrelated lockfile delta, including Gemini version regression. Doing nothing keeps the edit-guard stop in place and prevents final qualification and publication.

Approval permits exactly four official edit-guard accepts, after fresh diffs of the bound current hashes, followed by the bound targeted restoration and immediate records. If any current hash changes, stop without accepting or overwriting that file. The four current copies remain preserved. No other accept, reset, stash, restore, CI change or permission-policy change is covered.
