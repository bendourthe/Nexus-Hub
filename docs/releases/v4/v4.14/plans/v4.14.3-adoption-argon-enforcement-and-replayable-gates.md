## Overview

Google now lists `gemini-4-argon` as Stable and as its flagship reasoning model, and the maintainer asked that Nexus-Hub's records and recommendations support it. Phase 1 builds the profile through the existing writer, moves Google frontier to Argon in the routing snapshot, adds a provider-neutral fallback for users who cannot reach a frontier model, and re-stamps the Training page that embeds the map. Phase 2 records a verified vendor fact, Codex's 8,000-byte skill-prompt truncation, where any future Codex plugin work will find it. Phase 3 is the mandatory fail-closed final phase.

Success is observable: `model-map.py fallback` shows Argon at Google frontier, `stamp_training_models.py --check` passes, the profile validator and the strict freshness check pass with the new entry, and the read-contract carries the Codex entry.
