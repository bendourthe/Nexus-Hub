# v4.13.10 Phase R33 (Revision 9)

Maintainer review: the Talon's lance was a short beam that stayed on while fire was held, and some upgrades did nothing for it; every ship had to be reviewed for the same kind of flaw and for unfair advantages.

## Scope

- T107: the lance runs from the nose to the top of the arena (or to the first part of the boss it meets) and pulses while fire is held: lit 24 ticks, dark 36, Rapid cuts the dark time to 12; it ignites over 3 ticks and fades over 8, deals damage only while lit, and tapping fire cannot keep it on.
- T107: the Talon's card reads "A full-height ray that pulses" and its preview shows the full-height ray lit for 0.4 s and dark for 0.6 s.
- T108 Talon: Pierce overcharges the lance (half again the damage, a ray 2.4 times as wide) because the ray already passes through enemies; it does not burn through boss armour, and the boss's nodes and core take 0.4 of the lance's damage.
- T108 Warden: flak reached 180 px, could never touch the boss, and hit a mid-range target with one pellet in five; the cone is now tighter and heavier and reaches about 270 px, and Pierce now carries pellets to the second row.
- T108 Specter: single-target damage was 40 percent of the others' (orbs 2 every 22 ticks), and Pierce did nothing because a piercing orb never split; orbs hit for 4 every 18 ticks and a piercing orb splits on its first hit.
- T108 Raptor: under Rapid a new pull cut each burst short at the Sentinel form, so Rapid added a third; a pull now waits for the burst, and Rapid bursts come 2 ticks apart.
- T108 Missiles hit for 2 and seek live boss nodes; Wingman, Shield, Repair, Time slow, and Magnet were verified as working on every ship.
- T108 boss: the lower blade sat under the core and absorbed every straight shot from below, so the Vanguard, Warden, and Talon could not finish the boss; the blades now guard the core only while a node lives.
- T108 boss: the boss's sway clock ran from its spawn, so it snapped up to 200 px sideways on arrival; the clock now starts on arrival.
- T108 stats: Talon speed 1.3 to 1.2 (still the fastest), Raptor 1.05 to 1.12.
- T108 harness: `tests/guides/tools/game_balance.py` measures single, wave, reach, parked boss, and hunt values per ship and upgrade, and defines the band; the engine gained test-layer hooks `spawnDummy`, `configure({ immune })`, `state().dealt`, `state().boss.alive`, `state().weapon.pulse`, and `playerShots[].lit`.
- The design doc's "Revision 9 (R33)" section records the decisions, the band, and the measured table.

## Troubleshooting

- The first stationary boss measure parked the ship at the bottom; the Warden's flak and the old lance could never reach the boss, so the measure moved to the front of the ship's zone.
- A burn-through Pierce on the lance let the Talon destroy the boss in 12 s, about a fifth of any other ship's time; it was replaced by the overcharge.
- The stationary boss measure mostly hit the shielded core (0.5 to 1 damage per second for every ship); tracing a Vanguard run showed the lower blade absorbing every shot at the exposed core, which led to the blade fix.
- The hunt pilot first lined the ship's centre up on an upper node's open edge, which favoured single-gun layouts; it now lines up the outermost main gun, read from the rounds just fired, and tries three standoffs so homing orbs can curve in.
- The pilot also read Wingman and Spread rounds as gun lanes, which made those upgrades look slower on the boss; it now reads only main-gun rounds within 16 px of the ship.
- The lance-armour test first found no stop because the boss had snapped sideways on arrival, which exposed the sway bug.
- Updated test: `test_each_ship_fires_its_own_weapon` now expects orb damage 4 instead of 2, because the Specter's orbs were made heavier.

## Verification

- New tests: the lance reaches the arena top; it pulses with no lit run longer than 24 ticks and deals no damage while dark; Rapid shortens the dark time; tapping cannot keep it on; it stops at the boss's armour; the boss does not jump on arrival; the core is open from below once the nodes fall (Vanguard and Talon); single DPS, wave DPS, hunt time, and power index sit in the band; every weapon upgrade changes a measured value by at least 15 percent on every ship.
- Balance result: single DPS mean 13.6 (-11.5 to +15 percent), wave mean 19.8 (-30 to +35 percent), hunt mean 7.3 s (-22 to +24 percent), power index 0.97 to 1.03, smallest upgrade effect +28 percent.
- `NEXUS_REQUIRE_RENDER=1 python -m pytest tests/guides -q -p no:cacheprovider`: 737 passed, 1 skipped.
- `python scripts/stamp_guide_shared.py --check`: 21 fragments match in 2 pages; `training.html` is 461,935 bytes, under the 900,000-byte ceiling.
- Captures at 1440 x 900, clipped to the arena: the lance lit, dark, and fading, with and without Rapid, with Pierce, against the boss, each ship with Twin and with Spread, the selection screen, and four frames of the Talon card.
