## What this phase was for

`/review changes` can emit draft review comments and review a single validated commit, and it never posts.

## Steps taken

1. **T418, the draft format.** Added `multi-agent-code-review/references/draft-review-comments.md`.
    - It defines a never-post rule covering command-line clients, HTTP calls, and MCP or connector tools.
    - `CONFIRMED` is mapped onto the skill's own vocabulary: Stage 6 `validation: confirmed` at 75 or more, or confidence 100 from deterministic evidence. Drafting therefore runs Stage 6 even in interactive mode.
    - The output format has drafted entries, a "Not drafted (PLAUSIBLE)" table, general notes, and an explicit empty case.
    - Text safety covers fenced evidence, stripped HTML and images, and redaction. Destination rules cover the chat or a named path, no overwrite, containment, and no symlink following.
    - The skill links it from Stage 7.
2. **T419, the `commit` sub-mode.** Added to Stage 1. It sets the regex, `rev-parse --verify --end-of-options`, argument-array invocation, and the normal, root, and merge cases, plus a hostile-argument table. `review.md`'s `changes` scope names `changes <sha>` and carries the no-posting note.
3. **T420, the visual diff explanation** (nice to have, delivered). Added a `diff` scope on `/visualize`, `inline-visualization/references/diff-explanation.md` linked from the skill, and a one-line pointer from `multi-agent-code-review`.
4. **T421, tests and exercise.** `tests/skills/test_multi_agent_code_review_drafts.py` has 11 tests; a negative control made the posting check fail when a `gh pr comment` line was injected. The exercise is in [`v4.13.9-review-drafts-exercise.md`](../v4.13.9-review-drafts-exercise.md):
    - every hostile argument is rejected with 0 `git` calls
    - the normal, root, merge, missing, and ambiguous cases behave as documented
    - a real review of `4f4681e75` produced three `CONFIRMED` drafts and one `PLAUSIBLE` row
