## What this phase was for

At the handoff threshold (99% by default, `NEXUS_HANDOFF_THRESHOLD`) of a tracked usage window, the agent is told in its own platform's hook protocol to finish the current step and hand off, and a turn that ends without doing so gets one continuation.

## Steps taken

1. **T030**: `catalog/hooks/usage-guard.py` reads the shared probe inside a 5-second budget and renders per platform: Claude Code and Codex use `hookSpecificOutput.additionalContext` and a Stop `decision: block`; Cursor uses `additional_context` and `followup_message`; Copilot uses `hookSpecificOutput` on PostToolUse and Stop. Gemini CLI, Qwen, and Kimi payloads are recognized and stay silent; unrecognized platforms stay silent. A window is announced only when a message is actually sent, notifications are keyed on the window and its reset time and cleared after a reset or a drop below the clear line, every over-threshold window gets at most one continuation per turn end, and a platform continuation flag counts only when the previous continuation was the guard's own.
2. **T031**: registered in `catalog/hooks/settings.json` (UserPromptSubmit, PostToolUse, Stop), derived into Codex `hooks.json` with its timeout, Cursor's native `hooks.json` (with `_usage_probe.py` in the curated list), and Copilot's `nexus-hub.json`; Windsurf and Antigravity stay unregistered. The Copilot compatibility wrapper now passes `timestamp` and `stop_hook_active` through and adds `hookSpecificOutput` for PostToolUse.
3. **T032**: the Cursor compatibility wrapper's passthrough is pinned by `catalog/hooks/tests/test_cursor_hook_compat_passthrough.py`.
4. **T033**: `catalog/hooks/tests/test_usage_guard.py` (137 tests) and `tests/integrations/test_usage_guard_delivery.py`.
5. **Completion gate (decision 5)**: `scripts/completion_gate.py` lets a turn end without counting a refusal or writing a blocker only when `.nexus-hub/handoff.md` has a usage-limit header newer than the gate's last refusal AND the probe confirms a tracked window at or above the threshold; a fake handoff with the probe below the threshold is refused as before. The completion contract's "Usage-limit handoff" section owns the rule; `completion-gate.sh` and `.ps1` export their hook directory, and copies inside the repository are refused.
6. The probe gained a single-flight fetch lock and an in-process failure backoff; the guide counts were restamped (339 skills, 36 hooks) and the hook count updated in `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, and `data/marketplace.json`.

An independent review found a guard that went silent when a window first crossed during another hook's continuation, no re-warning after a window reset, the guard and the completion gate blocking the same turn end with opposite instructions, a continuation skipped when the leading window changed, and no single-flight probe fetch. All five were fixed with regression tests.
