## Steps taken

1. **T022, the boss geometry spike.** The plan expected to trace the logo PNG by hand. The guide already holds an exact vector of the mark (the shared `nexus-mark` symbol), so the boss uses its coordinates directly: crossing bars, four end nodes, a white core, two side plates, and two blades. That makes the boss a faithful logo by construction. The geometry and its gameplay roles are recorded in [`v4.13.10-game-design.md`](../v4.13.10-game-design.md).
2. **T023, the art.** Every entity is now drawn in code:
    - three ship forms with gradients and engine flames;
    - three enemy types with distinct silhouettes, the lancer's emitter warming before it fires;
    - seeded irregular asteroids with shading;
    - glowing shots, power-up capsules with readable icons, and particle explosions;
    - shield and upgrade rings, and a three-layer parallax star field with two faint nebulae.

    Particles come from a render-only random stream, so they never shift gameplay. Reduced motion slows the stars and drops glows, flicker, and most particles, without changing play.
3. **T024, power-ups and levels.** These are switched on per game: the buggy game has neither, the partly fixed game has progression, and the fixed game also has the boss.
    - **Levels:** they advance on survival time, level 2 at 90 s and level 3 at 200 s, and each brings a new ship form (Scout, Fighter, Sentinel) with a visible upgrade moment. The hitbox never grows.
    - **Enemies:** the lancer joins at level 2.
    - **Power-ups:** 14 percent of destroyed enemies drop one, from the `drops` stream. A shield absorbs one hit. Twin shot lasts 10 s, and the Sentinel gets a three-way spread. An extra ship adds a life, capped at 5.
    - **HUD:** it names the form and any active power-up.
4. **T025, the boss.** It arrives 4 min 20 s into the fixed game (3,600 ticks into level 3).
    - **Nodes:** its four nodes are turrets with aimed fire.
    - **Defences:** its plates, blades, and bars absorb shots, and the core stays shielded until every node falls.
    - **Victory:** destroying the core fires `bossDefeated` and ends the run as a victory.
    - **Shortcut:** a "Jump to the boss" button under the fixed game starts level 3, with the boss arriving 150 ticks later.
5. **Found by testing.** The boss countdown was decremented on the same tick it was set, so the boss arrived one tick early; the countdown now runs before the level check. A test-only `view()` (threat positions) was added for a scripted dodging bot.
6. **T026.** Added `tests/guides/test_training_progression.py` (17 tests): features per game, levels and forms by survival time, the boss at 15,600 ticks, the partly fixed game levelling up without a boss, the buggy game with no progression or power-ups, enemy variety by level, each power-up, two pickups in one tick, expiry across a level change, the Sentinel's twin and spread fire, boss nodes before core, plates absorbing shots, `bossDefeated`, the jump path, the defect schedule untouched by progression, and a dodging bot reaching the boss on two seeds.
