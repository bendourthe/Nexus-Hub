## Scope

- Strings set by the maintainer: the label reads "NEXUS BOSS DEFEATED" and the H2 reads "Your Reward: Download the Nexus AI Studio"; the prize paragraph, the download links and their targets, and the installer note are unchanged.
- The six-card slideshow is replaced by an animated Nexus AI Studio window built in HTML, CSS, and inline SVG, modelled on the Nexus AI source (read only, nothing copied): the frameless title bar with the mark, "Nexus AI Studio", and window controls (`TitleBar.tsx`); the sidebar tabs Chatbot, Agents, Images, Videos with their history segment, Settings, and the GPU status card "GPU usage" (`Sidebar.tsx`, `types/modules.ts`, `GpuStatusFooter.tsx`); the per-pillar accents, dark surfaces, and type stacks (`styles/tokens.css`); the composer placeholders (`ChatPage.tsx`, `CodingPage.tsx`, `MediaComposer.tsx`); the "Rendering..." and "Crafting..." progress labels; and the agent tool names `list_directory`, `read_file`, `edit_file`, `write_file`.
- One clock plays four scenes of 7.6 s each: Chatbot (a parsed PDF and a streamed answer from Gemma 4 12B), Agents (a Nexus Hub harness strip whose skills, hooks, and plan mode load, then a plan and four tool cards on Qwen3-Coder 30B-A3B), Images (a prompt, a 20-step progress bar, and a picture resolving from noise on SANA 1.5 1.6B 1024px), and Videos (a prompt, 8 frames crafted, then a clip playing with a timeline on Wan 2.2 TI2V 5B). Model names come from the app's `core/registry/catalog.json`.
- Every scene shows "Local on this device" in the header, the model in the composer picker and GPU card, and "On this device" on the finished result. A cursor clicks the sidebar tab, the composer, and send, and the prompt is typed.
- On phones the sidebar becomes the app's icon rail, the plan card and the context pill are hidden, and the meta stacks. Reduced motion holds the finished Agents scene with no cursor. The loop and its CSS spinners run only while the panel and the tab are visible.
- The mockup is `aria-hidden` with a one-line text alternative under it; the panel keeps its single H2.

## Troubleshooting

- The first capture clipped the image card and the agent's final message; the window body grew from 520 to 590 px (560 on phones) and the image card now sizes to its picture.
- The video meta never appeared because its visibility toggle was missing; it now shows when the clip is ready.
- In reduced motion the cursor stayed visible because `.nxs svg{display:block}` outranked `.nxs-cur`; the rule is now `.nxs .nxs-cur`.
- On phones the stacked meta hid "On this device" in two-item rows; only a middle item is hidden now.
- The edit guard flagged training.html from a stale record; `git status` showed the file clean at HEAD c51e0edab, so there were no user edits to carry forward.
