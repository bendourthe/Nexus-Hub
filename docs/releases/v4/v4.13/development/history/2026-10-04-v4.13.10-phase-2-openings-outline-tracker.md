## What this phase was for

Give the content pages one opening style and a clickable outline, and give Training a stage tracker built from the Home page's loop.

## Phase entry

Queue re-assessment: no plan landed on `develop` since Phase 1, so nothing drifted.

## Steps taken

1. **T006, the opening.** `guides/website/shared/opening.css` defines one opening: a centred title in the Home hero-subtitle style with one gradient phrase, a one-paragraph description, and a full-width visual map of glyph tiles that link into the page. It extends the existing Foundations opening (a deliberate v4.4.2 choice that a test pins) rather than replacing it. Training's intro uses it with a six-part journey map.
2. **T007, the outline.** `shared/outline.css` and `shared/outline.js` (`window.NexusOutline`) render a sticky "On this page" bar under the site header. It runs in two modes. "scroll" mode, for Foundations and Cheatsheets, follows scrolling, jumps in place, moves focus to the section heading, and updates the hash with `replaceState`, so it never routes. "route" mode, for Training, follows the stage. On phones the bar collapses to one line naming the current entry, and choosing an entry closes it.
3. **The byte ceiling moved the guide adoption.** Adding the opening, outline, and visual maps to Foundations and Cheatsheets took the guide to 506,118 bytes, over its 500,000-byte ceiling, while the old Training (about 104 KB) must stay until Phase 7. The guide edits were reverted. The edit script that made them is kept at [`v4.13.10-guide-openings.py`](../v4.13.10-guide-openings.py) and becomes Phase 7 sub-task 7.4a (T042), after the removal frees room. `stamp_guide_shared.py` gained per-page fragment lists, so the guide carries its original fragments and Training carries all of them.
4. **T008, the tracker.** Training shows the Home loop's six steps (same commands, same labels, same order, checked by a test) with done, current, and not-yet states. In the second loop, `/describe` shows as "already mapped" and links to review. It exposes `setStage(loop, step)` and `markDone(loop, step)` and is hidden on the intro, where the journey map already shows the whole path.
5. **Fixes found by rendering.** The first phone render overflowed to 662 px and hid the gradient phrase. The guide's mobile-menu rules and gradient variable lived outside the shared CSS. The mobile-menu block became the `nav-mobile-css` fragment (a marker-only change to the guide), and the opening states its gradient directly. A scroll test then showed Training ignored reduced motion for smooth scrolling. The page-independent half of the guide's reduced-motion block became `motion-css`, carried by Training now and adopted by the guide in Phase 7.
6. **T009.** Added `tests/guides/test_page_openings_and_outline.py` (tracker order against the Home loop, the intro opening, outline highlight, click, keyboard, phone collapse, scroll mode without routing, tracker states for five stages, no horizontal overflow at 1280 and 390 px in both themes) and two stamp tests for per-page fragment lists.
