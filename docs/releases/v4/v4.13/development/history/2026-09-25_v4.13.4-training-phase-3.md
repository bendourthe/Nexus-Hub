## 2. Chronological Steps

### 2.1 Preserve the size ceiling

The Phase 2 guide measured 498,421 normalized bytes, leaving 1,579 bytes for Phase 3. The embedded room WebP was 820x461 although its SVG slot was 640x360. Re-encoding it at the slot's dimensions and WebP quality 50 reduced the inline guide by 6,492 bytes, to 491,929 before painter edits. Browser captures at 640x360 and 2x device pixels preserve the room's composition and recognizable objects with a modest reduction in fine texture: [before](../training-rebuild/phase3-room-before-2x.webp) and [after](../training-rebuild/phase3-room-after-2x.webp). No ceiling increase was made.

### 2.2 Distinct painters

`paintEnemy` now dispatches by archetype. The Interceptor has a forward-swept dart, the Gunship a wide hull and paired weapon pods, the Lancer a long hull and charging emitter, and the Drone a rounded unarmed chassis. Seeded asteroid outlines and crater positions use the existing `look` value. The player has a plated hull, visible intake, input-responsive afterburner and eased bank; fixed-mode invulnerability retains its original alpha flicker.

Phase 2 introduced bolt, spread and beam behavior but omitted projectile paint from the Phase 3 task list. The new painter makes the spread triangular, the beam warning dashed and dim, and the active beam solid and wide. The model's warning interval and collision timing did not change.

### 2.3 Render and inspect

The real Training route loaded without page errors at 1440 and 420 px, with a playable canvas of 330x439 and 320x426 CSS pixels respectively: [desktop](../training-rebuild/phase3-arena-1440.png) and [narrow](../training-rebuild/phase3-arena-420.png). A temporary probe called the guide's own painters, then was discarded; its [desktop](../training-rebuild/phase3-specimens-1440.png) and [narrow](../training-rebuild/phase3-specimens-420.png) captures show all archetypes, two equal-size rock seeds, both beam states and the thrusting player. The four silhouettes remain distinct without relying on color. A separate [damage-mode frame](../training-rebuild/phase3-damage-flicker.png) shows the fixed-mode ship visibly dim during invulnerability while buggy mode stays opaque. In the unmodified route, a direct `play` fixture reached tick 220 while running with two enemies and two asteroids, no page errors, and this [live canvas capture](../training-rebuild/phase3-live-play.png).
