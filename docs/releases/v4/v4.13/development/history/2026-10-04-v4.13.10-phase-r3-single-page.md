# Session History -- v4.13.10 Phase R3: one scrolling Training page

**Date**: 2026-10-04
**Plan**: [v4.13.10-training-page-rebuild](../../plans/v4.13.10-training-page-rebuild.md), plan revision after the maintainer review
**Phase**: R3 (T048, T049)

## What this phase was for

The maintainer wanted Training on one scrolling page, not stages switched one at a time with Continue buttons.

## Steps taken

1. **Every stage is a section of the page**, in workflow order and anchored by its own id, so `#loop1/review` and every older hash scroll to the section. The page has one `h1`; stage titles are `h2`.
2. **Each loop opens with a header** listing the Home loop's steps; in loop 2, `/describe` is marked already mapped. The single tracker and the intro's step strip were removed, as were the Continue links.
3. **The controller tracks the stage being read.** `NexusTrainingPage.go()` lands a stage at once for scripts and tests; a reader's links scroll smoothly; the rail follows the six parts. Games pause by themselves when scrolled out of view.
4. **Found by testing**:
    - `behavior: "auto"` followed the page's `scroll-behavior: smooth`, so jumps landed mid-scroll; scripted jumps now use `"instant"`.
    - Step sections had no scroll margin and landed under the header.
    - A scroll during a jump's pin was lost; it is now re-checked when the pin ends.
    - The "already mapped" label, now always visible, measured 3.8:1; the command is dimmed instead of the whole card.
    - Several R2 figure animations kept running under reduced motion, because `:nth-child` rules outranked the stop rule; one figure-wide rule now stops them.
