## What this revision was for

The maintainer found revision 2 "almost there". The remaining requests were:

- **Rail**: a ring that is never clipped.
- **Training opening**: an IDE in the middle panel, ships that match the game, and a buggy panel that makes the bug impossible to miss.
- **Game**: a fuller game with ship choice, glowing upgrade carriers, density that grows within a level, wormholes, and a map per level.
- **IDE**: highlights that guide the reader's eye.

The maintainer also renamed the game Nexus Defenders and shared reference images for the ship designs.

## Steps taken

1. **R12, the rail ring.**
    - **Cause**: the current entry's ring (a scaled node with a 4 px halo) reached past the rail's clipped edge.
    - **Fix**: the rail now pads its edge, and the track moves with the nodes.
    - **Test**: a new check measures the ring against the rail on all three pages at 1280, 1440, and 1920 px; it fails on the previous page (ring at 18.75 px, rail at 24 px).
2. **R13, the Training opening.**
    - **Middle panel**: a mini IDE (explorer, editor, chat). Its chat lights the six commands in turn while the editor strikes the buggy line and adds the fix, and the model switches from Claude Opus 5.5 to GPT-6.1 Sol at the handoff.
    - **Ships**: drawn in lit steel with cyan edge lights.
    - **Buggy panel**: the hit shows "-6", the bar drains to zero, the ship explodes, and the figure holds for about two seconds on "The bug: 6 damage emptied a 100-point bar".
    - **No spoiler**: there is still no boss or logo.
3. **R14, the game** (built on branch `wip/v41310-game2`, commit `35274b5ee`, then brought in file by file).
    - **Start screen**: four ship designs inspired by the references (Vanguard, Raptor, Talon, Specter) with stats, and a colour key for ten upgrades.
    - **Carriers**: enemies and asteroids that carry an upgrade glow in its colour and drop it.
    - **Within a level**: spawn density rises as the level goes on.
    - **Between levels**: a wormhole sequence, and a distinct map per level (Cyan Reach, Violet Halo, Ember Belt, Nexus Station).
    - **Buggy fatal hit**: it shows its real damage and drains the bar to zero, matching the opening.
    - **Name**: Nexus Defenders on screen; internal identifiers are unchanged.
4. **R15, highlights.**
    - **Marks**: an animated outline with a label marks each generated file, edited block, and reply's key content (its first table, code block, or list).
    - **Timeline**: marks are timeline actions, so seeking lands on the same box from either direction.
    - **End state**: each step's last reply stays marked at the end.
    - **Story**: steps and replies carry their mark labels, and the schema allows them.
