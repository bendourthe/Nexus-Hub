# v4.13.10 Phases R35 to R37 (Revision 10)

Maintainer review: enemies died to one hit, the ship was held to the lower half of the arena, the levels felt alike, pickups carried no risk, only the stars moved, and the Nexus boss arrived and fell with no ceremony before the reward panel opened.

## Scope

- T110: every enemy class but the interceptor has a life bar, always shown; health grows with bulk (radius over cruise speed) and with the level: gunship 7/9/10, lancer 8/10/11, bomber 12/15/17, agent 7, interceptor 1.
- T110: no class but the interceptor dies to the heaviest single round of any ship (a plasma orb, 4) or to one trigger pull of any ship at 170 px; asteroids keep their breakup.
- T111: the ship flies the whole arena, stopping below the HUD band (66 CSS px, 98 on a phone, 128 on a phone while the boss bar shows); `state().player.top` reports the limit.
- T112: pace 80/60/50, enemy speed 1/1.15/1.25, health x1/x1.25/x1.4; on level 3, 30 percent of the larger classes arrive behind a 5-point shield ring (measured 32.1 percent); the first-shot grace and the aimed fire are unchanged.
- T113: four downgrades (Mine, Freeze 2.5 s, Slow fire 5 s, Scramble 4 s) ride on hazard carriers marked in violet and red with a warning badge; each has a HUD chip with a timer and a badge over the ship; the start screen key lists them under "Hazards".
- T113: a quarter of carriers hold a hazard, and the carrier rates rose (enemies 0.26 to 0.34, rocks 0.3 to 0.4) so upgrades drop about as often as before; the magnet never pulls a hazard, and `view()` lists hazard pickups as threats.
- T114: `bgMotion()` drives both renderers: star layers scroll, the nebula drifts, the planet, sun, or station sways on a slow loop, the station turns, and planet bands flow; `background()` exposes the offsets.
- T115: the boss enters through a rift for 270 ticks (90 under reduced motion) with a camera shake and a "NEXUS" title card; the ship cannot be hurt and shots pass through until it arrives; the spawn tick is unchanged.
- T115: a NEXUS life bar (56 points: four nodes of 8 and a core of 24) spans the top of the arena; two agent ships launch every 300 ticks (up to six, only with threats on), weave, fire aimed darts, and carry pickups 45 percent of the time.
- T116: the kill starts an 8 s finale: chain blasts and energy cracks across the scorched wreck, a final blast with shockwaves, a portal that pulls the ship through, "The New Dimension" with a star tunnel, and the prize card "Reward unlocked: Nexus AI Studio".
- T116: the finale's last frame emits `rewardUnlocked`, and the page now opens the reward panel on that event instead of `bossDefeated`; Space, Enter, Escape, a click, or a tap skips (once to the prize card, twice to the end); reduced motion shows three still frames over 230 ticks.
- New engine hooks: `skipFinale`, `spawnMine`, `spawn(type, x, y, carry, { shield })`, `enemyClasses`, `difficulty`, `downgrades`, `dropRates`, `background`, `lifeBars`, and `state()` fields `foes`, `hazards`, `downs`, `mines`, `downgrades`, `chips`, `hud`, `finale`, `agentsLaunched`, `shieldedSpawned`, `largeSpawned`, and `boss.hp`, `boss.intro`.
- The design doc's "Revision 10" section records the class table, the per-level table, the downgrade table and drop rates, the finale timeline, and the re-measured balance band.

## Troubleshooting

- The first class values (gunship 6, lancer 7, agent 5) let a Warden flak volley kill a gunship or an agent with one pull at mid range (three pellets for 6); gunship and lancer went to 7 and 8, the agent to 7.
- The first level-3 tuning (pace 44, speed 1.3, health x1.5) let the dodging bot reach the boss on 8 of 10 seeds; easing level 3 alone did not help, and a diagnosis showed no downgrade involved: with hazards taking a quarter of the carriers and tougher enemies dying less often, repairs dropped too rarely, so the carrier rates were raised.
- With full-arena movement the balance harness's boss pilots flew into the boss; they now hold the R33 standoff line (55 percent of the arena height) and steer to it while waiting out the wormhole and the entrance, which the wormhole would otherwise undo.
- The first destruction capture looked like a pale, healthy boss with small pops; the wreck is now scorched and flickering with energy cracks, the chain blasts are denser and grow, and the final blast adds two shockwave rings.
- The boss bar's value overlapped the bar's right end, and HUD marks for the title card survived into the next frame; both were fixed after the captures and tests showed them.
- The mine first read as a pink dot; its mesh is larger and its spiked icon is drawn over it.
- Updated test: `test_the_end_card_sits_in_the_centre_of_the_arena` now calls `skipFinale()` twice before probing the victory card, because the end card waits for the finale; without the skip it passed vacuously against a hidden overlay.
- Updated test: `test_the_start_screen_fits_the_arena_with_no_scroll_bar` also checks the hazard legend's four items and title, because the start screen gained them.
- The full suite caught the Slow fire legend colour (#c026d3) at 4.25:1 against the key's background, under the WCAG AA 4.5; it is now #e879f9.
- Updated test: `test_density_grows_through_a_level` now sums twelve seeds with a 1.25 floor instead of one seed with 1.4; the single seed sat on a noisy edge (1.5 before, 1.38 after, because longer-lived enemies fire more and their fire draws on the spawn stream), and twelve seeds give about 1.35 in both the R33 and the R35 builds. A cap-retry change tried first changed nothing and was reverted.
- Updated harness: `tests/guides/tools/game_balance.py` boss pilots hold the R33 standoff line instead of holding "up", because the ship can now fly the whole arena.
