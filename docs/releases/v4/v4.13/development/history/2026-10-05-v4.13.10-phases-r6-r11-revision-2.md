**Date**: 2026-10-05

## What this revision was for

The maintainer accepted the direction of the Foundations opening and asked for a second rework of Training:

- **Pages**: polish to all three pages.
- **Training structure**: one pass of the six-command Development Workflow, with no loops.
- **Agent steps**: VS Code-like IDE animations driven by a cursor, with replay, seeking, and speed.
- **Providers**: a provider handoff during `/implement`.
- **Game**: a WebGL game with a health model.
- **Opening**: a Training opening that does not give away the boss.

Decisions taken with the maintainer:

- WebGL 3D in code.
- Six commands, including `/update`.
- Both seeded bugs kept.
- A usage-limit handoff from Anthropic to OpenAI.

## Steps taken

1. **R6, guide polish (committed earlier).**
    - The rail sits centered in the free margin.
    - The Foundations figure moves one dot left to right, fills its prompt lines with an example, and shows the harness and platform items as plain labels.
    - Home names the Development Workflow.
2. **R7, one pass.**
    - **Story version 3** (`src/training-story.json`, schema updated):
        - Stages are intro, buggy game, describe, review, plan, implement, test, update, and the reward.
        - The review finds both bugs, and one two-phase plan fixes them.
        - The sample project now has a health bar and a damage table.
    - **Heads**: each part opens with the six-command workflow strip, marking done, now, and next.
    - **Notes**: the play notes are plain type between hairlines; the tinted callouts, badges, and provider buttons are gone.
    - **Old links**: `#loop1/*`, `#loop2/*`, and `#play-partial` land on the matching part, and the guide's Training links now use the new ids.
3. **R8, the IDE player** (`src/training-story.js`, rewritten).
    - **Layout**: each step is a VS Code-like IDE:
        - title bar;
        - activity bar;
        - explorer;
        - editor with tabs and breadcrumbs;
        - chat with the model and effort pickers in its composer;
        - status bar.
    - **Timeline**: the step's script compiles into timed actions. The cursor picks model and effort, types the command, and presses Send. The agent's steps appear while files are typed in or edited line by line, then a Markdown reply.
    - **Rendering**: each frame is rebuilt from the script for its time, so seeking lands on the same frame from either direction.
    - **Controls**: play and pause, replay, a seek bar, and 0.5x, 1x, and 2x speed.
    - **Playback rules**: a step plays when half of it scrolls into view. A seek counts as a start, so autoplay never overrides scrubbing. Reduced motion shows the finished frame.
    - **Model names**: models show as names (`modelName`: Claude Sonnet 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol), never as ids.
    - **Handoff in `/implement`**:
        1. The usage bar reaches 100 percent.
        2. A limit notice appears, and the cursor runs `/handoff`.
        3. The agent writes `.nexus-hub/handoff.md` and replies with a resume prompt; the cursor copies it.
        4. A new chat opens, the cursor picks GPT-6.1 Sol, pastes the prompt, and Phase 2 finishes on OpenAI.
        5. `/test` and `/update` stay on OpenAI.
    - **Model stamp**: `scripts/stamp_training_models.py` now reads the tiers from each step's model picks.
4. **R9, the opening figure.**
    - A cursor plays the buggy game, where one 6-damage hit destroys the ship.
    - It then runs the six commands, while the model switches from Claude to OpenAI.
    - Finally it plays the fixed game: the same hit costs 6 health.
    - No boss or logo appears. The old journey figure and its CSS are removed from `shared/opening.css`.
5. **R10, the WebGL game** (built in a sibling worktree, branch `wip/v41310-game`, commit `45c40bf97`, then brought in file by file; only the engine and its two test files changed).
    - **Rendering**: hand-written WebGL with lit meshes, plus a 2D fallback.
    - **Health**: hull and shield bars, with damage set by source and asteroid size.
    - **Enemies**: four classes with distinct weapons, and asteroids that break up.
    - **Difficulty**: Easy, Medium, and Hard.
    - **Defect**: `firstHitFatal` now kills on any hit.
    - The design note `v4.13.10-game-design.md` gained a revision-2 section.
6. **R11, tests.**
    - **Story tests**: rewritten (21 tests). They cover:
        - schema and order;
        - commands;
        - both bugs in one review;
        - the handoff script;
        - IDE parts and model names;
        - seek determinism;
        - controls;
        - autoplay and reduced motion;
        - the live edit;
        - carry-forward;
        - the workflow strip;
        - plain notes;
        - no "loop" wording.
    - **Updated for the new ids and classes**: the page, openings, sweep, lifecycle, browser matrix, render tool, Foundations heading, catalog-command, and model-stamp tests.
    - **Deep-pass contrast fixes** in the IDE: the placeholder, the "Preset" label, and the line numbers (also on an added line) now meet 4.5:1.
