## A measurement error worth recording

The first contrast sweep reported 11 light-theme failures at 2.99:1. That was a defect in the sweep script's background walk-up (it fell through to transparent and scored against black), not in the page. Re-measuring against the real painted background showed the light theme already passing at 6.20:1. No token was changed on that bad signal; only the four genuine findings above were touched. Recorded because "fixing" a mismeasured color would have been a silent regression.
