Observation to queue to analyst to archive

Four stages, three edges, two unbound parameters

The process has four stages and three transitions. Incoming observations enter the queue, an analyst reviews them, and approved records are archived. Every label below is taken from the sources; nothing between the stages is inferred.

Source: README.md, prior handbook heading

What the sources say about each stage

  • stage 1 of 4

    Observation: Field observation arrives

    An incoming observation is the unit of work. The sources do not describe how observations are captured or transported.

  • stage 2 of 4

    Queue: Holds at most 24

    Admission is decided by admitted(queue_size), a strict comparison against queue_capacity = 24. Once 24 observations are held, the next one is refused.

  • stage 3 of 4

    Analyst review: Human review step

    An analyst reviews queued observations. The sources describe review as a step and name its outcome, but do not describe review criteria or ordering.

  • stage 4 of 4

    Archive: Approved records only

    Approved records are archived. The sources state no destination for records that are not approved.

What each edge is constrained by

  • Observation to Queueadmission gated by queue_capacity = 24
  • Queue to Analyst reviewno ordering or selection rule is stated
  • Analyst review to Archiveoutcome for records that are not approved is not stated

Configured but not bound to a stage by any source

  • retry_limit = 3The README says configuration controls retry attempts, but no supplied module implements a retry. The stage this binds to is not stated.
  • timeout_seconds = 8The README says configuration controls a timeout, but no supplied module implements a timer. The stage this binds to is not stated.
