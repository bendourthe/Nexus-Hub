# Session History - v4.11.2 Phase 3

**Date**: 2026-09-13
**Plan**: [v4.11.2 document and deck quality](../../plans/v4.11.2-adoption-document-and-deck-quality.md)
**Phase**: 3 - Figure and diagram construction gates
**Branch**: `feat/v4.11.2-document-deck-quality`

## What this phase set out to do

Gate the figure rules that carry crisp signals - legend integrity, tick range,
stroke consistency - and crop the dead space the source project's user asked to
remove at least ten times.

## Steps

1. Added three checks to the audit: `legend-entry-not-drawn`, `tick-outside-range`, `viewbox-dead-space`.
2. Built four fixtures, including a corrected counterpart for the crop.
3. Ran the matrix. It failed in two ways, both instructive.
4. Extended the suite to 29 tests.
5. Wrote the rules into their two owning references.

## Troubleshooting

### My hand-written crop was wrong

`viewbox-cropped.html` was supposed to be the corrected counterpart to
`viewbox-dead-space.html` and it still failed. I had cropped it by eye to
`0 20 600 210`, which left 14% dead space at the bottom.

Rather than adjust the threshold until my crop passed - which would have been
fitting the gate to my mistake - I read the crop the check itself suggested and
applied it verbatim. It passed.

That turned into an assertion worth keeping:
`test_the_suggested_crop_is_actionable_not_advisory`. The check does not merely
complain; it emits the fix, and the fixture proves the fix works. The fact that
the computed suggestion was right where my eyeballed one was wrong is the
argument for emitting it at all.

### The new check failed eight older fixtures, including both defences

`viewbox-dead-space` fired on every Phase 1 fixture, because they all declared
`0 0 600 300` regardless of where their ink sat. Two of those are the
false-positive defences, which are worthless the moment they report anything.

The question was whether this was a false positive or fixture debt. The evidence
settled it: the real repository handbooks passed throughout. So the check was
right and my fixtures were sloppy. Each was re-cropped using the check's own
suggestion, restoring one-defect-per-fixture across all thirteen.

## A deliberate rule violation, commented at the call site

`viewbox-dead-space` uses `getBBox`, which this audit's own docstring forbids.
The ban exists because `getBBox` ignores transforms and disagrees with the
screen. Here the comparison is entirely in user space - content bounds against a
user-space `viewBox` - so transforms are not part of the question. Anything
judged against the screen still uses `getBoundingClientRect`.

I commented the exception where it happens rather than in a changelog, because
the next reader to see `getBBox` in this file will be reaching for the delete
key.

## What went right

Treating the check's suggestion as the authority over my own eyeballed crop.
The instinct to adjust a threshold until my fixture passed would have quietly
weakened the gate to accommodate an error of mine.
